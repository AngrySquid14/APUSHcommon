#### Why Did The English Colonies Have An Increased Reliance on the Atlantic Slave Trade in the 1700s?
- Post Bacon's Rebellion (1676), English colonies switch to African slavery for tobacco labor.
	- Before 1700s, most labor was from indentured servants
	- Why? Demand for colonial goods in England was too high for indentured servant labor to meet, due to soil exhaustion plantations were now larger, and lack of indentured servants after *Bacon's Rebellion (1676)*
	- New England colony had less slaves due to having small farms
#### What Confrontation Occurred with the Powhatans (1622)?
- Opechancanough War/Powhatan War/Jamestown Massacre in 1622
	- Powhatans kill 1/3 of white settlers in Jamestown in surprise attack
	- Prompted by increased expansion into native land by settlers
#### Largest Slave Rebellion in English Colonies
- Stono Rebellion (1739) AKA Hutchinson's Revolt
	- ~20 Armed African slaves set out for Ft. Mose (first free black town, those who make it will be freed by Spanish)
		- Leaders mainly Congolese, some with military training
	- Burn down plantations while drumming
	- Fail to reach Ft. Mose, armed militia catches up, rebels end up captured and murdered, decapitated heads posted up on sides of roads
	- Led to harsh(er?) slave codes/laws
