**Salutary Neglect** (1700-1750)
- Hands-off approach to governing the colonies
	-  Unofficial British policy during King George I (1714-1727) & King George II (1727-1760)
	- *Causes of Salutary Neglect:*
		- British prosperity from increasing trade-more lax colonial rule as a result
		- Need for Britain to focus more on European affairs
		- Self interested, incompetent officials, declining enforcement
			- **Prime Minister Robert Walpole** (1721-1742) fills British gov. (including *Board of **Trade**) with "do-nothing political hacks"*
	- *Effects of Salutary Neglect:*
		- Rise of self-government in 13 colonies
			- Strengthen powers of **representative assemblies**
			- Increased self-rule → colonial autonomy increases → American identity unique from Britain
- **Mercantilism** 
	- Mercantilism Goals
		- Increase wealth
		- Don't lose $ to foreign competition
		- Provide market to sell goods
		- Unimpeded source of raw materials
	- *Navigation Acts* (1651-1751)
		- Mostly Obeyed Acts:
			- ex. Act of 1896 - prevented fraud, create vice admiralty courts
		
		- Extensively Violated Acts:
			- ex. Iron Act (1750) - prevented manufacture of finished iron products
			- ex. Molasses Act (1733) - cut American imports of molasses from French West Indies
				- Why? Colonies rely on molasses for rum
		- England prevents colonies from trading with any other nation but them
			- Colonies smuggle supplies to trade with other colonies and nations, defying **Navigation Acts**
				- Why? English trades for only fur, tobacco, timber, colonies produce other products (fish, wheat, etc.)
				- Other nations pay higher prices for American goods
- **Dominion of New England**
	- Massachusetts Bay colony disobeying Nav. Acts, not accepting Anglican (Church of England) church, etc.
