## Battle of Gettysburg (PA)
- In Gettysburg, Pennsylvania
- *General Lee's* army of 77,500 meets Union army
	- Union army sends 90,000 troops
- *3 day battle* (July 1st-3rd, 1863)
### Significance/Results
- *50,000* Casualties
	- **Bloodiest battle ever fought on U.S. soil** => not easily won
- <u>major Union victory</u>
- <u>Last battle fought on <i>Union soil</i></u>
- ***Turning point of the Civil War***
## Battle of Vicksburg (MI)
- on Mississippi River (Vicksburg, Mississippi)
- Grant plans to take Vicksburg
	- Grant captures Mississippi capital, Jackson
		- => control of main rail line into Vicksburg => cut off supplies
	- Places Vicksburg under *siege*
### Significance/Results
- Part of Union's **Anaconda Plan**
	- Gain control of Mississippi River => cut South in half
- Fall of Vicksburg => last southern stronghold on the Mississippi surrenders => Confederacy/South splits in 2
- early 1864 - *Lincoln puts Grant in charge of entire Union military effort*
## Sherman's March to the Sea
- *General William T. Sherman* - Known as "Sherman the Brute" by Southerners, General under Grant
- Sherman sent by Grant to Atlanta
- *September 2nd, 1864* -> Sherman forces people out of Atlanta, then burns city to the ground.
- *Sherman's 62K troops trek 250 mi -> "March to the Sea"*
	- End in Savanah, GA
	- <u>goal was to destroy Southern resources</u>
		- **Employed a "Total War" strategy
		- Burnt buildings, twisted railroad tracks (Sherman Bow ties), generally destroyed Southern resources
### Significance/Results
- Showed that the Union could hurt the South, instilled fear
- Take Savanah, GA
- Ended war faster b/c brutal tactics + made Southerners want to end the fight
	- Southern wives write to husbands begging them to return home and end the war
## Why the Union Won the War
- **1864 Election**: Lincoln(Republican) vs McClellan(Democrat)
	- Lincoln criticized for <u>suspending habeas corpus, taking too much power, issuing Emancipation, and for making the war last long</u>
	- McClellan sought peace settlement to end war
		- Democrats generally united on peaceful end to war, hence "Peace Democrats"
	- *Lincoln wins election and is reelected => no hope of peace settlement with Confederacy/South*
- ***Why the Union Won***:
	- North
		- Greater Technology, Population, + Resources
		- New advantages -> Fearless Military Leaders (Grant, Sherman)
			- Steady leadership of Lincoln
			- Emancipation Proclamation (New war cause to end Slavery)
	- South
		- Unsustainability -> Used up resources, lack of new troops + supplies, Infrastructure destroyed (Think: Sherman's March)
		- <u>Couldn't gain European allies because of Emancipation Proclamation</u>
