## Battle of Gettysburg
- In Gettysburg, Pennsylvania
- *General Lee's* army of 77,500 meets Union army
	- Union army sends 90,000 troops
- *3 day battle* (July 1st-3rd, 1863)
### Significance/Results
- *50,000* Casualties
	- **Bloodiest battle ever fought on U.S. soil** => not easily won
- <u>major Union victory</u>
- <u>Last battle fought on <i>Union soil</i></u>
- ***Turning point of the Civil War***
