### John Brown's Raid at Harpers Ferry
- **Goal**: Capture South's arsenal at Harpers Ferry, then arm slaves and rebel
	- Largest weapons stockpile in the south
- Brown & men overwhelmed b/c *not aided by any slaves*
	- U.S. Military gets involved, Robert E. Lee shows up
- <u>Harpers Ferry raid fails</u>, Brown's sons are killed, and he is charged with treason
	- *John Brown receives death sentence in 1859*
## John Brown's Court Address
- **Arguments**:
	- That he<u> did not intend to murder</u> during the raid on Harpers Ferry
	- That he was trying to just move slaves through the country to freedom
	- That he <u>did not intend to start a slave rebellion</u> or insurrection
		- That he did not want to commit treason
	- That he <u>does not deserve the death penalty</u>, because if he had done the raid *"(on) behalf of the Rich, the Powerful, the Intelligent...it would have been all right."*
	- Uses the Bible to argue his actions would be justified by it
- **Goals**:
	- Make himself a martyr, likely so his message would spread
	- Give final words (he accepts the death penalty as he says he has done right)
## 1860 Election
- ### Candidates
	- **Abraham Lincoln**:
		- <u>Republican</u>, IL
		- *Platform*: <u>No Slavery in territories</u> (*NEW LANDS*)
			- Slavery shouldn't *extend*, not that South has to give up slavery
	- **Stephen Douglas**:
		- (Northern) <u>Democrat</u>, IL
		- *Platform*: <u>Popular Sovereignty</u> should decide the issue of slavery when territories become states
	- **John Bell**:
		- <u>Constitutional Unionist</u>, TN
		- *Platform*: <u>Federal Gov. should support slavery</u> and *also* <u>defend the union</u>
	- **John Breckinridge**:
		- (Southern) <u>Democrat</u>, KY
		- *Platform*: <u>Federal gov. should <i>protect</i> slavery</u>
- ### Election Outcome
	- **Lincoln**: 59% Electoral Vote, 40% Popular Vote
		- <u>Did not get a single Southern vote</u>
	- **Douglas**: 4% Electoral Vote,  29% Popular Vote
	- **Bell**: 13% Electoral Vote, 13% Popular Vote
	- **Breckinridge**: 24% Electoral Vote, 18% Popular Vote
- ### Election Effects
	- South outraged b/c President election w/o Southern vote
