## Kansas-Nebraska Act
- Bill authored by Stephen Douglass (Illinois)
	- proposed a bill to create new territory (Railroad) in Midwest to get Southern vote
	- let residents decide slavery (*popular sovereignty*) and split in 2 (*Nebraska & Kansas*)
	- repealed the **Missouri Compromise** as it potentially allowed slavery above 36-30 line
- ### Bleeding Kansas
	- Voters decide slavery -> pro and anti-slavery forces flood Kansas to vote
	- Violence breaks out
		- Lawrence (a free-state town) sacked
			- Abolitionist **John Brown** retaliates

## Slavery Defense and Divides
### Competing Slavery Arguments (Brinkley)
- ### Northern Arguments
	- Slavery dangerous b/c it threatened white economic opportunities
	- Believed Democracy required *property ownership rights*, *control over one's labor*, & *access to personal advancement* (**Free Soil & Free Labor**)
	- Saw South as a *closed, static society*
		- also upholding aristocratic system
	- Thought slavery would extend and destroy northern capitalism (**Slave Power Conspiracy**)
- ### Southern Arguments
	- Developed in response to growing abolitionist movement & prosperous cotton econ.
	- Religious justification 
	- Saw North as greedy and morally corrupt
	- Claimed slavery was essential to southern life
	- Claimed African Americans were not able to be citizens
## Compromise of 1850
- ### Texas & the Mexican Cession
	- War w/ Mexico
	- Provision which wouldn't allow slavery in future territory from Mexico
		- Passed in House of Representatives (Northern-controlled)
		- Failed in Senate (Southern-controlled)
			- Highlighted *intensity of the slavery divide*
- ### Compromise of 1850 Summary
	- #### Issues:
		- California wants to become Free State
			- would upset 15-15 balance of Free/Slave States
		- Texas (Slave state) in debt, disputing Gov. over their Northwestern border w/ New Mexico + Utah
			- NM & UT likely to become free states
		- Northerners getting closer to banning slavery in capital (DC)
	- #### Solutions:
		- Senator Henry Clay makes 5-prong compromise, passed by congress
		- California admitted as free state *without adding a slave state*
			- As balance, California agrees to send 1 pro-slavery and 1 anti-slavery senator to Congress
	- #### Outcomes:
		- **California gets to be free state**
			- Clear Northern victory
		- **NM & UT decide slavery status by *popular sovereignty***
			- Moderate victory for both
		- **Slave trade (NOT SLAVERY) ended in Washington, D.C.**
			- Moderate victory for North
		- **Congress pass new strict *fugitive slave law***
			- Clear Southern victory
		- **Texas gives up claims to NM for $10 million**
			- Moderate victory for South
	- #### How did tensions increase?
		- **Fugitive Slave Act**
			- Any citizen (North or South) could capture runaway slaves
				- Even runaway slaves on free soil
				- Citizens could be deputized and be *required* to hunt down slaves
			- Legally allowed African Americans accused of running away to be kidnapped
			- Abolitionists became extremely unpopular, many times lynched after act put in place.
