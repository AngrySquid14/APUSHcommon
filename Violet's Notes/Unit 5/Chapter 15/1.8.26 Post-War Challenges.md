## Effects of the Civil War on Southerners
- Return from war to find destroyed homes and farms
	- Many Southerners dislocated, homeless
- Some believed it was punishment from God, beliefs shaken
- Some still believed Southern fight in Civil War was noble, and that the South would be redeemed
## End of Civil War = African American Freedom?
### U.S. Government: Provides Rights
- ***13th Amendment***: Abolishes Slavery
	- Abolishes involuntary servitude nationwide, <u>except as punishment for a crime</u>
- ***14th Amendment***: Citizenship
	- Cannot deny citizenship based on race, creed, color, or former conditions of servitude
- ***15th Amendment***: Right to Vote
	- Cannot deny right to vote based on race, creed, color, or former conditions of servitude
- ***Civil Rights Act of 1866***: Military Reconstruction Act (1867)
	- Provide resources for former enslaved - schools, aid, etc.
### The South: Segregate, Deny Rights & Power
- 13th Amendment -> ***Sharecropper System***
	- Former slaves end up working on cotton farms for former masters under a contract
		- Paid half of cotton profit, are given a home and tools to pay off
		- Result: *generational debt*
- 14th Amendment -> ***Black Codes & Jim Crow Laws***
	- **Black Codes**-> State Laws limiting black rights
		- *Mississippi Black Codes* (1865-66)
			- Restricted rights of "Freedmen, free negroes, and mulattoes"
			- **Examples**:
				- Apprentice Law 
					- -> <18, if orphan or parents don't provide, court has authority to place them (with preference towards former *owners*)
					- -> former owner has power to beat child
				- Vagrant Law
					- -> if neglect employment, "misspend" money, "don't provide" for family => deemed <u>vagrant</u>, fined up to $100/10 days in jail
					- -> "unlawfully assembling" => fined, up to 10 days in jail
					- -> whites assembling or "associating" (fornication and adultery) w/ blacks, mulattoes => mulatto women deemed vagrants, fined, up to 6 months in jail
				- Penal Laws
					- -> no trespassing, <u>insulting actions</u>, assaults, preaching w/o license, selling alcohol => fine, jail up to 30 days
		- **Laws were open to LOTS of interpretation by White Govs., juries, and judges**
		- Free African Americans feared lynching, unable to confront injustice
	- **Jim Crow Laws** -> <u>Segregation</u> - public facilities
		- Named after Black song and dance character from 1830's *Minstrel shows*
		- Denied citizenship rights + equality from *14th Amendment*
- 15th Amendment -> ***Poll Tax, Literacy Test, Grandfather Clause***
	- Literacy Test -> many former slaves failed b/c never learned to read & write, & tests were exceptionally difficult
	- Grandfather Clause -> if father was slave, no voting right -> 1st free generation can't vote
- 15th Amendment -> ***KKK***
### Black Americans: Fight for Their Rights
- ***Establish Own***: Churches, Colleges, Clubs, Volunteer Fire Depts.
- ***Ida Wells Barnet***: Anti-Lynching Campaign
- ***Black Vote***: Elected 14 to House + 2 to Senate (an entire state)
- ***Boycott***: Form NAACP
	- National Association for the Advancement of Colored People - Founded in 1909
