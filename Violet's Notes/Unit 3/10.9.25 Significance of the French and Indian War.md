##### Albany Plan of Union (1754)
- ###### Purpose was to negotiate a treaty between...
	- Iroquois + the British
- ###### Significance:
	- "Join or Die"
	- Colonial unity
	- Did it pass?
	- **Delegates of convention passed it, colonies and Britain rejected it. Why?**
	- Increased taxes by colonists
	- Colonies didn't want to surrender control
- ###### Salutary Neglect:
	- Britain left colonies to develop on their own, so long as they were profitable
##### The War
- ###### Causes:
	- Started in 1754 and ended in 1763
	- George Washington sent to challenge French expansion
- **F & I War part of larger conflict (7 Years' War)**
- Most Natives (except Iroquois) sided with the French
- British forcibly enlisted colonists and forced colonists to provide shelter
##### Peace of Paris, 1763
- Britain gained all French land east of Mississippi River, *save New Orleans*
	- French gave NO and land west of Mississippi to the Spanish
##### Big Ideas After the War
- **Balance of Power shifts in NA**
	- Native Americans lose a partner in France
- Tensions increase between Britain and colonists
- Britain feels the colonies should share in the responsibility of the empire
	- Colonists want to go back to *Salutary Neglect*
##### Political Impacts of the War
- **Salutary neglect ends**
	- Britain now takes hands-on approach to governing colonies
	- King George III and new PM George Grenville
- **Proclamation Line of 1763**
	- Hoped to avoid conflict between colonists and Natives
	- British troops stationed on border
	- Colonists resent not being allowed to expand
- **Colonial resistance to British Policies**
	- Sons of Liberty
	- Smuggling
		- Writs of Assistance, Vice Admiralty Courts (Part of Nav. Acts, *British* courts without jury)
			- Guilty until proven innocent in Admiralty Courts
##### Economic Impacts of the War
- **Britain is in debt**
- Colonists contributed little financially, Britain began to tax the colonists, and strictly enforced those taxes\
- *End of Salutary Neglect*
	- Currency Act
	- Stamp Act
- *Resistance:*
	- Stamp Act Congress
		- Boycotting of British Goods
	- "No taxation Without Representation"
##### Social Impacts of War
- **Colonial soldiers saw selves as equals w/ British soldiers. not vice versa**
	- British viewed colonial soldiers as inadequate
	- George Washington had limited promotion
- Emergence of a colonial identity
- **Colonial Resentment, Unite AGAINST British Taxes**
	- Stamp Act Congress
##### Cause of British-French conflict?
- Expansion of British colonies moving into interior of NA (Ohio Valley)
- Colonial migrants intruded onto Native American lands, disrupt entrenched trade between French + American Indians
##### Impacts of the War?
- **Britain majorly expanded, gained *massive* amounts of land**
	- French essentially removed
	- Native Americans lose a key ally and trade partner
- Britain becomes *in debt* from war - seeks to raise revenue, gain more control over colonies
	- Britain begins to collect taxes - Sugar Act (1764), Stamp Act (1765), etc.
##### Post 7 Years' War, Britain sought to limit expansion
- Britain forbade expansion west of the Appalachian Mountains - Proclamation line (of 1763)
- Natives resisted the encroachment of colonists
	- Pontiac's Rebellion (1763)
##### Pontiac's Rebellion (1763)
- *Major uprising against British colonial power* in the Great Lakes Region after 7 Years' War, land encroachment, + changing trade policies
- Led by Ottawa Chief Pontiac, alliance of tribes attack British forts + settlements
	- Ultimately result in military stalemate, prompting Britain to issue the Proclamation of 1763
		- Set boundary west of Appalachians to prevent further conflict, but led to increased colonial resentment + further divide between British settlers and Native peoples
### Skill Practice
	"And We do hereby strictly forbid, on Pain of our Displeasure, all our loving Subjects from making any Purchases or Settlements whatever…… without our especial leave and License for that Purpose first obtained. And We do further strictly enjoin and require all Persons whatever who have either willfully or inadvertently seated themselves upon any Lands ……. to remove themselves from such Settlements."
**-Proclamation Line of 1763**
###### 1) What was the reaction to this document?
-  Anger, disobedience
	- Colonists more resentful, don't follow Proclamation
###### 2) What was the cause of this document?
- Conflicts with Native Americans (Pontiac’s Rebellion)
	- Resulting military stalemate forces Britain's hand
###### 3) What were the effects of this document?
-  Distrust, Colonists resent and defy the British Act
- More tension between Natives + British settlers

### Key Takeaways
##### Overall Significance of the War?
- Large territorial gain, as France is now expelled
	- Loss of key Native American ally.
- Britain in huge *debt* after war
	- Tax colonists to pay of debt
	- Salutary Neglect ends - no more "hands-off" approach from Britain (Authoritarian rule)
- **Tensions from the 7 Years' War helped *lead to* the *American Revolution***
