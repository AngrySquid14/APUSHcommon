#### What United Colonists against the British
- British taxes W/O colonial representation or consent
	- Parliament created taxes w/o colonial representation, claims colonies are "virtually" represented
	- Colonists were OK w/ colonial taxes if they had representation and could vote for it
- Colonists *successfully* boycotted taxes (Stamp, Townshend) and developed more resolve in their cause.
##### How did colonists justify their resistance?
- Rejected "virtual representation" (idea that Parliament acted in best interest of all British subjects)
- Right of Individuals:
	- Taxation, writs of assistance, admiralty courts
- Enlightenment Ideas:
	- consent of the governed, "life, liberty, and property"
- Local traditions of Self-rule:
	- colonists had ability to elect colonial representatives
