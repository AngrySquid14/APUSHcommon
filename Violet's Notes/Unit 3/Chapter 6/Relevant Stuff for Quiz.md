### **Common Sense** by Thomas Paine
- Pamphlet that became a bestseller, convinced 1000s to support revolution
	- *Appealed to common people*
	- Attacked king, used reason + **Enlightenment Ideas** to argue for separation from the Crown
- About 500,000 copies printed (of a population of 2.5 million) helped turn the tide for Revolutionary Fervor.
- Afterwards, colonists *no longer wanted* to stay in British Empire

### Significance of Shays Rebellion
- Exposed **Articles of Confederation** as creating too weak of a government
### **NORTHWEST ORDINANCE**
- Strength of **Articles of Confederation**
- **The Northwest Ordinance** assisted in orderly expansion of the United States, outlining a plan for applying for statehood to western territories
		- 5k *free males who own 50 acres* (1 acre = a football field) can start govt
		- Population of *60,000* could become a state
		- <U>Promised education, freedom of religion, trial by jury, and no slavery</U>
### Federalists
- Want *strong national government* so the republic will survive
- <u>Support constitution</u>, acknowledge it isn't perfect
- East coast, *wealthy* elites, *more educated*
- Convinced by *Shays' Rebellion* that the central government needed more power
- *George Washington, Ben Franklin, James Madison*
- Want single person to lead executive branch
### Anti-Federalists
- <u>Don't support constitution</u>
- Concerned over individual rights being at risk, don't want another king
- Demand for a *Bill of Rights* to be added to constitution
- Wanted important political powers to **remain with the States**
- *Rural, Poor*, Western
- *Thomas Jefferson, Sam Adams, Patrick Henry, Richard Henry Lee*
