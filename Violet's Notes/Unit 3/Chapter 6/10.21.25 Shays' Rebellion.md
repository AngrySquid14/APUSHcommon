### Shays' Rebellion
- <u>1786</u>, Daniel Shays & 1,200 other poor farmers join together
- <u>Jan 1787</u>, attacked weapons storage in Massachusetts
	- Thought gov. was too weak to act
- *Poor farmer protesters storm courts having cases against farmers, causing them to close*
- Stormed jails, removed prisoners
- **Governor of Massachusetts seeds state militia**, resulting in <u>4 protesters dying in fight</u>
#### Causes
##### Daniel Shays
- B/c Inflation, Continental soldiers didn't receive pay earned fighting in the war (Daniel Shays was a soldier)
- He couldn't afford tools & seeds for planting, was a farmer
##### Problems for Farmers
-  Post-Revolutionary War Economic Depression
	- States made taxes high to pay debts
		- Only paid in gold or silver
		- Former soldiers in debt b/c not paid
		- Those who didn't pay had land taken, sent to prison
##### Farmer's Demands
-  *Poor farmers not represented in State Legislatures (who pass debt laws*
	- Want to get rid of prison for debtors
	- Want law to pay debts w/ crops
	- Want paper money (farmers had no gold or silver)
- *State Legislatures did <u>NOT</u> listen*
##### Reasons for Shay's Rebellion
- Farmers required to pay debts in gold; are poor and not represented in mass legislature.
- Wealthy lawmakers invested money in war, want reimbursement from farmers debts
#### Outcomes
- **Federal Government was *powerless* to end the rebellion
	- exposes weakness of first US government to entire world
	- prompts leaders to create a *stronger central government*
	- <u>1791</u>, Poor farmers elect officials who support their stance (closing courthouses & demand financial help from Congress)
- **The thing Samuel Adams Said
	- "Rebellion against a king may be pardoned, or lightly punished, but the man who dares to rebel against the laws of a republic ought to suffer death"
#### Views on Shays' Rebellion
- Samuel Adams writes law, **Riot Act
	- Prohibits 12+ people from meeting, *gives government power to shoot rioters*
	- Very hypocritical coming from Adams
- *Thomas Jeffersons's Response:*
	- "A little rebellion now and then is a good thing. It is a medicine necessary for the sound health of the government. God forbid that we should ever be twenty years without such a rebellion."
