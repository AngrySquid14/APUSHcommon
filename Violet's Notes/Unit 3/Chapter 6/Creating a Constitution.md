### Challenges?
- Agreed Executive (chief) should be *elected* & <u>NOT a monarch</u> (No Kings)
	- Challenge: Executive power- one person or committee?
		- Agreed 1 person, but limited to 4 year term
- Challenge: Who selects representatives to congress, state legislatures (gov. leaders) or people?
	- Agreed election by both people **(House)** and stale legislatures **(Senate)**
	-  *amendment #17 (1913), People vote for Senate Directly*
- Challenge: How many reps for each state in congress?
	- *Virginia plan*: Based on population (VA-largest state), causes small states to lose
	- *New Jersey plan*: Each state gets 1 vote (NJ-9th largest), causes small states to lose.
	- ###### Result: Connecticut/Great Compromise: House based on population, Senate - 1 Rep per state
- Challenge: How to select President? Who best to select? *(Distrust of voters' ability to make good decisions)*
	- State Legislatures choose "electors" who meet to make selection for each state. # of electors = to # of reps in House. *(Electoral College)*
- Challenge: House of Reps based on population, how are slaves counted into population?
	- South wants to count all or won't sign the constitution
	- North thinks slaves can't be both property and people
	- **Three-Fifths Compromise**: African slaves each count as 3/5 of a person for representation # s
- Challenge: North wants ban on importation of slaves, but many think it'll end soon anyways, *not worth risking South's vote for constitution*
	- Congress can regulate tax &trade imports, *but not tax exporting or end importing slaves UNTIL* **1808** (in 20 years)
