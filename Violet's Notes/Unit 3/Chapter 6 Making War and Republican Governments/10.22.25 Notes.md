#### Federalists
- Want *strong national government* so the republic will survive
- <u>Support constitution</u>, acknowledge it isn't perfect
- East coast, *wealthy* elites, *more educated*
- Convinced by *Shays' Rebellion* that the central government needed more power
- *George Washington, Ben Franklin, James Madison*
- Want single person to lead executive branch
#### Anti-Federalists
- <u>Don't support constitution</u>
- Concerned over individual rights being at risk, don't want another king
- Demand for a *Bill of Rights* to be added to constitution
- Wanted important political powers to **remain with the States**
- *Rural, Poor*, Western
- *Thomas Jefferson, Sam Adams, Patrick Henry, Richard Henry Lee*
#### Intellectual Debates
- *Alexander Hamilton* & *James Madison* primary writers of Federalist Papers
	- Papers outline principles of Federalists
- Federalists ensure ratification of the Constitution by promising to add *Bill of Rights*
	- *James Madison* drafts **10** amendments to Constitution which become BoR
### The Ratification Process
- Anti-Federalists demand Bill of Rights
	- Basic rights need to be spelled out
- **9 out of 13** States needed to *ratify Constitution*
- Framers called for state ratifying conventions, instead of state legislatures b/c <u>legislatures would lose power</u> if Constitution ratified
- Ratification process *began in* **Fall of 1787**
