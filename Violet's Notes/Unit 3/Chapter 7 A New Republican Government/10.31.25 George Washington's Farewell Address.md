#### Term Limit
- **Washington leaves office after 2 4-year terms**
	- There was no *term limit* at the time
		- Washington was done being president after 8 years
	- Set precedent for presidents to serve for up to 2 terms (except Franklin Roosevelt)
### Washington's Farewell Address
- Concerns for our Union?
	- Need for a government that serves the whole
	- Division between North and South, East and West?
- Role of the Constitution?
	- Until changed by the people, the Constitution is "sacredly obligatory" over all"
	- Right of people to start government comes before personal duty to obey government
	- accepts Constitution being altered
- View of foreign Nations?
	-  wanted to keep U.S. separate from foreign affairs
	- "The nation which indulges towards another a habitual hatred or a habitual fondness is in some degree a slave...."
- Concern with political parties?
	- Tells people to avoid aligning themselves with political parties
