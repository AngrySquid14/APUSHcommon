### **Britain (coalition) vs France War** (1793-1798)
-  America neutral, allowed trade w/ both sides of war
	- <u>Avoided being openly allied w/ Britain or France</u>
	- **Ties with Britain**: trade for Tobacco, cotton
	- **Ties with France**: trade for W. Indies Sugar, <u>Support during Am. Rev.</U>
- War disrupted European Farming
	- Wheat prices rise from 5-8 shillings, remained high for 20 years
	- War created boom in profits for wheat growers (**Chesapeake & Mid-Atlantic regions**)
- **Cotton** - w/ cotton gin & mechanized cloth production in England, profits in **S. Carolina & Georgia** were boosted
- <u>Americans take over French West Indies sugar trade</u> ($20 mil/year profit)
- Turmoil in Europe increased agricultural prices, accelerating Western Expansion
	- Farmers took advantage of new western lands
##### Federalists (Hamilton, Washington, Adams, Jay) Support:
- Merchants, creditors
- Wheat exporters - *Chesapeake region*
##### Republicans (TJ, Madison) Support:
- Southern tobacco & rice farmers
- Indebted western farmers
- *New England* - Small farms, 
- Southern backcountry - Scotts, Irish, Germans
### Belligerent's responses alter popularity of the ideology/political positions
#### Citizen Genet Affair - *(French Ambassador to U.S.)*
- April 1793, arrives in *Charleston S. Carolina*
- Recruits + arms American "privateers" (non-navy attack vessels)
	- uses them to attack British ally, *Spain*, in Florida
- Sec. of State - TJ, denies Genet's request to end neutrality
- Genet defies Washington, *captures British ships & rearms them as privateers*
	- Washington sends 8k work complaint, <u> Jefferson and Hamilton agree on stopping Genet</u>
	- Washington demands France recalls ambassador (Genet)
		- Genet fears being executed by guillotine back in France, *begs, and Hamilton convinces Washington to grand him asylum in U.S.*
		- ***This event weakened pro-French supporters***
#### Jay's Treaty
- **1795, British seize 250 Am. cargo ships w/ French sugar, etc.
- Washington dispatches **John Jay** (Federalist Papers, diplomat) to Britain
	- Jay makes treaty w/ Britain, Treaty...
		- 1) accepts Br.'s right to *stop neutral ships*
		- 2) *Pay off pre-revolutionary war debt to British*
		- 3) Allows American claims for illegal searches
		- 4) Remove British troops & Indian agents from *NW Territory*
		- **Senate Barely ratifies treaty by 2/3 majority**
###### <u> This event reflects the Federalist-powered government's pro-British policy & weakens popular support for Britain </u>
### XYZ Affair
- **Response to Jay's Treaty w/ Britain, France seizes Am. ships in W. Indies**
- Adams, to avoid war w/ France - sends 3 delegates to France
	- *Charles Pinkney - Ambassador to France, John Marshall, Elbridge Gerry*
- **French Foreign Minister Charles de Tellegrand** sends 3 *French* delegates
	- Jean Hottinger - **X**, Pierre Bellamy - **Y**, Lucian Hauteval - **Z**
	-  Delegates ask for bribe of 50k pounds & loan before negotiating
- Adams required to report details to Republicans - *replaces names with X,Y,Z*
- Congress cut trade w/ France (**1798**) & authorize privateers to attack French ships in W. Indies
	- <u>Note: Later Treaty of Mortefountaine (1801) establishes preferred trading privileges to France & sincere peace</u>
- *Pro-French Americans and Irish protest XYZ*
######  President Adams responds w/ 3 acts limiting free speech & immigration
- **Naturalization Act** - lengthens *Am. citizenship* from 5 to 14 yrs
- **Alien Act** - Authorizes *deportation* of foreigners
- **Sedition Act** - Prohibits publication of *anti-government sentiments*
###### <u> This event pushes support back to Britain and damages the Federalist image - lose 1800 election </u>
