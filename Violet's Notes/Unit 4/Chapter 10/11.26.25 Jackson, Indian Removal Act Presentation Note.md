### Jackson & Native Americans
- Jackson grew up in Tennessee (Western Frontier)
	- Natives and settlers often fought
- Forced Natives to give large amounts of land
- Fought Creeks and Seminoles in the South
- *Jackson saw removal as necessary for economic growth*
	- Natives had fertile land
### The Indian Removal Act of *1830*
- Forced Native Am. to relocate <u>west of the Mississippi</u> (**Oklahoma**)
- President could "exchange" lands west of Mississippi for land that natives occupied in Eastern U.S.
	- Federal gov. could *forcibly remove tribes from their ancestral lands*
- ***Results***: Creek & Choctaw nations most affected, *by **1837**, 46k Natives removed*
	- Cherokee fought removal the longest, eventually forced to leave by gunpoint
		- Forced to march on foot (**Trail of Tears**)
#### Johnson V. McIntosh (1823)
Private citizens <u>could not</U> purchase land from Natives
	- b/c it was not theirs to sell
	- b/c the land belonged to the U.S. and their "right to discovery"
### The Trail of Tears (*1830's*)
- Forced removal of:
	- *Cherokee*, Muscogee, Seminole, Chickasaw, Choctaw
- **1838**, Cherokee - last to leave, suffer from harsh winter
- Many starved and died from the cold
### Native Resistance
- Seminoles (Florida) fought back (*Second Seminole War*)
- Cherokee took case to Supreme Court (*Worcester v. Georgia*)
- <u>Some leaders signed treaty in hopes of protecting their people</U>
- Natives protested - petitions, letters, delegations, etc. to Washington DC
- *After*: Natives rebuilt communities in Indian Territory (*Oklahoma*)
	- Tried to preserve culture
