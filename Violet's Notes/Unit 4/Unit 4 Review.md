### American System: (Henry Clay)
#### Purpose?
- U.S. industries struggle to compete w/ British goods (post War of 1812)
- 1st BUS expired, *2nd BUS rechartered in 1816* for 20 years
- Infrastructure weak, esp. in WEST (KY, OH, TN)
- *Purpose of American system was internal improvements, rechartering BUS and keeping up with demand*
#### Components?
- **BUS**
	- 1st BUS opposed, 2nd allowed in 1819
		- Andrew Jackson -> veto 3rd BUS
	- Henry Clay advocated for 3rd bus to *stimulate economy*
- **Internal Improvements**
	- *Interstate* (between state)
		- Railroads
		- Funded by National gov.
	- *Intrastate* (within a state)
		- Erie Canal
		- Previously, Fed. Gov. only funded Interstate developments
			- State gov. pays for Intrastate
			- *American System* -> want to fund both
	- Many opposed improvements (Andrew Jackson) (James Monroe)
		- Monroe vetoed Erie Canal 
- **Tariffs**
	- <U>Used to pay for internal improvements</u>
	- Tax on foreign goods
	- *Tariff of 1816, 1828, & 1832*
		- Raised prices of foreign goods
			- Resistance to 1828 and 1832 tariffs (*Nullification Crisis*)
#### Success? Failure?
- **Successes**
	- North + Midwest -> closely linked
		- Aided by infrastructure
	- Infrastructure increases
		- -> American industries prosper
	- 1st of many Government-Sponsored programs
- **Failures**
	- Didn't unify all of U.S. (South)
	- Sectionalism increases (*nullification crisis*)
	- -> Continued debate abt role of federal gov. in intrastate business

### The Age of Jackson: *Tariffs*, *Nullification Crisis*, *Bank War*
#### Issues of Each?
**Tariffs**
- Tariff of Abominations (1828)
	- Andrew Jackson partially elected to respond to it
	- Tariff raised rates to ~40-50%
	- Favored Northern industries, hurt South
**Nullification Crisis**
- North liked tariff b/c -> protected domestic industries post 1812 War
	- deemed necessary to maintain U.S. industries
	- tariffs provided revenue
	- tariffs = produce war material for U.S.'s defense
- Opposition to *Tariff of 1828* (**Tariff of Abominations**) by South (*SC*)
	- SC hated tariffs b/c -> higher prices for manufactured goods
		- Europe also suffered b/c less money spent on European imports
	- <u>Opposed b/c South received no tariff protection for their goods</u>
- Tariff of 1832 lowered rates from 1828 tariff
	- However, South *still opposed tariff* b/c it didn't lower rates enough
	- SC calls state convention, votes to *nullify* tariff, *threatens secession* if gov. tries to collect tariffs forcefully
- Henry Clay creates *Compromise Tariff of 1833*
	- <u>-> tariff would fall by 8%/year for 8 years</u>
		- South approved compromise
	- *Force Bill* also passed same day, allowed Pres. to use military to collect tariffs
**Bank War**
- Beef w/ Henry Clay & Daniel Webster
- Jackson vetoes *bank rechartering bill*
	- "Tyrant King" label b/c of this
- Eastern Workers + Western Farmers opposed BUS, believed it would incr. prices
- Jackson: hard money (gold & silver) > soft money (dollar)
- 2nd Bank would benefit bankers + entrepreneurs in Boston, NY, Philly
- <u>Ultimately, National Bank prevented in 1836 by Jackson</u>
	- -> Henry Clay's American System is cooked
	- *Jackson wins bank war*, wins 1832 election
#### Relevance/ Continuities for Each?
- Nullification crisis is avoided
	- Results in *Compromise Tariff of 1833*, but no secession
- Bank war won by Jackson
	- American System Ends, 2nd National Bank destroyed
