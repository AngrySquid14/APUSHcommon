### Test Vocabulary
- "***Antebellum***" - Time period BEFORE a war (*Civil War*)
- "***Egalitarianism***" - Belief in equality of people
- "***Perfectibility of Man***" - Rousseau - man's ability to adapt/improve (trait-distinguishes himself from other animals)
- "***Unitarianism***" - Belief God is United as 1, as opposed to trinity (*The Father, The Son, & The Holy Spirit*)
- "***Transcendentalism***" - Ralph Waldo Emerson & Henry David Thoreau promoted improved world w/ individuality & nonconformity, feminism & communal living followed.
