### 2nd Great Awakening & Early Reform Movements (1790-1840)
- 1st Great Awakening was in the <u>early 1700's</u>
#### Causes:
- The *post-Revolutionary* rise of *democratic ideals* and individualistic beliefs-rejecting the rigid structures of traditional churches.
- <u>Rejecting Enlightenment reasoning</u> and Deism for a more emotional and personal expressions of faith
	- embracing Enlightenment reasoning + Deism were causes for *1st Great Awakening*
- Shift from Calvinist emphasis on *Predestination*, to focusing on free will and one's ability to choose salvation.
- The *Market Revolution* promoted opportunity and ability to control of one’s destiny resonated with salvation being reached by personal efforts to reform your life.
- *Westward expansion* led people to seek new spiritual connections away from the established churches in the East.
#### Results:
- Church membership (Baptists & Methodists) skyrocketed, and the 2nd Great Awakening leads to *movements to reform society ills*.
	- Reform Movements -> BIG DIFFERENCE between 1st and 2nd Great Awakening
#### Women’s Activism = *MIDDLE CLASS ORIGIN*
- **Moral Reform** - Prostitution, Single Factory Workers
- **Mental Illness** - Poor treatment, like criminals.
	- *Dorothea Dix* inspected hospitals and jails (Massachusetts) reporting on horrid conditions, spoke to the legislature to gain funding to improve hospitals and jails.
- **Temperance Movement** - Sought to ban or limit the availability of *alcohol*
	- Led by _ , concerned for <u>family neglect, crime, and sickness</u> caused by alcohol.
	- Major movement, will go on for a LONG time
- **Increased Rights** - Property, Inheritance, Education
	- Women petition and campaign to <U>own property, vote, have a public voice, and right to an education.</U>
	- *Some states* allowed married woman to *own property*, (NY Joint Guardianship) to sue in court, keep wages, and All-girl schools formed.
	- Voting Movement - **Seneca Falls Convention** : Elizabeth Cady Stanton, Lucretia Mott (Organizers) New York (*1848*) *Declaration of Sentiments* <u>"All men AND women are created equal"</U>
	-  Progress was slow and they faced fierce opposition (Women’s vote not until 1920 !)
#### Abolition of Slavery
- Movement to end the cruel treatment & system of slavery. Goes against our nation’s ideals of liberty.
- *African American Leaders* - (**David Walker**, **Nat Turner**) Christ an Avenging God of Justice, Rebellion is Just
- Anti-Slavery Societies, religious groups (*Quakers?*):
	- Arguments against slavery - 1) Unchristian, 2) Evil Conditions of Slavery
- Use of Press to argue for abolition
	- **William Lloyd Garrison** - publisher of The Liberator, a Boston Abolition Newspaper
		- Threatened for publishing abolition arguments
- Women- **Sojourner Truth**: a former slave, active speaker. 
	- **Harriet Jacobs** - <i>Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl</i>
#### Counter-Abolition
- Early movements: American Colonization Society- Liberia, Back to Africa Plan
-  “*Slavery as a Positive Good*” - John C. Calhoun
- “Bible defenses of Slavery” - Josiah Priest
- “An Affectionate Scene in Kentucky” – Richard Johnson
#### Public Education-
- *Juvenile delinquency* and crime showed a need for public schools. 
	- <U>Not seen as a government responsibility</U>
- Reform in 1830s pushed for *government-sponsored public schools* for democracy and American values.
- *Massachusetts* expanded state sponsored education. Campaigns expanded to other parts of country.
	- Resistance from families needing children for labor
	- Resistance b/c public schools = paying taxes for others.
