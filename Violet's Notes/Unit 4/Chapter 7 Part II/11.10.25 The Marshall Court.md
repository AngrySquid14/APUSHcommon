### Marbury v. Madison (1803)
- *John Adams' Midnight Appointments*
	- Appointed multiple new judges right before Jefferson's inauguration
		- John Marshall appointed to chief justice
		- Marbury appointed to a lower federal court
	- Jefferson administration refuses to honor appointment
		- Marbury appeals to Supreme Court, doesn't get granted by Jefferson
- Marshall fears constitutional crisis, sacrificed Marbury, create new power for Supreme Court
	- Marshall + Supreme court declare part of law granting Marbury appointment *unconstitutional*
	- Marbury denied judgeship, Supreme Court gained **Judicial Review**
- **Judicial Review**: Court's authority to review an action by gov. + determine if it is constitutional
