# Native Americans In the New Republic
- <u> Natives sought to limit American westward expansion</u>
- **Battle of Fallen Timbers** (1794-Ohio)
	- Natives led by Little Turtle were defeated
	- **Treaty of Greenville** -> Natives ceded significant amount of land (most of Ohio)
- <U>British alliances w/ Natives contributed to tensions w/ America pre-War of 1812</u>
	- ***The Constitution gave NO mention of the treatment/relations w/ Natives***
		- many treaties made, but not always enforced
			- ex. **Cherokee Nation v. Georgia** (*1831*) & **Worcester v. Georgia** (*1832*)
# Relocation of Natives
- <u> Beginning in the 1830s, Natives were pushed further and further west</u>
	- **Indian Removal Act, *Trail of Tears***
- Plains Indians
	- Predominantly hunted buffalo
	- Land & lifestyle was changed due to U.S. expansion
# *A Century of Dishonor* (1881)
- Written by *Helen Hunt Jackson* 
- Discussed relationship between U.S. Govt. and Native Am.
	- <u>Demonstrated that Natives were harshly treated and often deceived by the govt.</u>
# The Dawes Act (1887)
### Purpose
- Assimilate Native Am., eliminate their cultures
### Provisions of the Act
- Native tribes dissolved
- Heads of families received 160 acres of land
### Impact
- Many native children sent to boarding schools
- Native Am. lives changed -> hunting to farming
- <u>Most of Natives' land was lost</u>
