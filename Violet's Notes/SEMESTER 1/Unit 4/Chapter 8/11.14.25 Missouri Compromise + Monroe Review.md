
## Monroe Administration Review
- Post *War of 1812*
### Era of Good Feelings
- Expansion of economy
	- growth of white settlement
		- creation of new states (at ~22-25 states now)
	- growth of Nationalism
	- No international threats
	- Only one political party
		- death of Federalist party because they were irrelevant
		- Monroe brings in those from all over U.S. and political views/parties to unite country in government
	- Goodwill tour
		- Monroe travels across nation, term "era of good feelings" coined by Boston newspaper.
### Adams-Onis Treaty
- 1819 - Spain cedes all of Florida to the U.S.
		- this gives up claims north of the 42nd parallel in the PNW
		- America gives up claims to Texas
### Monroe Doctrine
- 1823 - U.S. decides to stay out of *European affairs*; Europe needs to stay out of Latin America
		- U.S. becomes a dominant power in the Western Hemisphere
## Sectional Divide
### A Nation Divided: The Issue of Slavery
- 1781 - U.S. adopts 1st government under Articles of Confederation
- 1783 - Treaty of Paris officiallyu ends the War for Independence w/ Britain
- 1784 - 5 Northern states adopt laws to end slaver
	- PA, NH, MA, RI, CT (Pennsylvania, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut)
- 1787 - Under Articles of Confederation, *Northwest Ordinance* prohibits slavery in Northwest Territory (Ohio River to Great Lakes)
	- Results in 6 free states (Ohio, Indiana, Illinois, Michigan, Wisconsin, Minnesota)
- 1788 - Constitution ratified, *3/5ths Compromise* made (3/5 slaves count for pop. determining representation # in Congress)
- 1789 - 8 slave states, 5 free states
- 1800 - 9 slave states, 8 free states
### The Missouri Compromise *(1820)*
- 1819 - Missouri requests to be admitted to the union as a slave state
	- At time, U.S. was balanced w/ 11 slave states and 11 free states
	- Missouri being admitted upsets balance -> Maine admitted as a free state
- Imaginary boundary line between future free + slave states made (*36'-30' Line*)
#### Impacts of the Missouri Compromise
- Balance remained at *12 free, 12 slave states*
- States admitted in *alternating* free & slave states in the short term
- Lasted *34* years
- As the U.S. expanded beyond the Louisiana Territory, slavery became #1 issue
	- New compromises/court cases would attempt to address issue
