### Embargo Act 1807
- Prohibited ALL foreign commerce (mainly France + Britain)
	- *Context*: Napoleonic wars, America wants to trade w/ both sides -> neither Fr. nor Br. wants the other to have trade w/ U.S.
		- Aimed to compel foreign powers to respect American rights
		- TJ wants foreign powers to *respect American neutrality*
	- *Goal*: Jefferson thought no U.S. goods = European war comes to a halt
		- *What actually happened*: Fr. and Br. found trade elsewhere, **Embargo Act failed**
			- Damaged U.S. economy greatly
			- Seen as excessive government, made Jefferson look like a hypocrite
- Goes against Jefferson's ideals of small government
	- U.S. naval vessels were used to patrol American ports, NOT small government
- Why prohibit trade?
	- *Agrarian vision* 
	- Detach from foreign countries
	- Not pro-British like federalists
- **Benefits?**:
	- stimulated domestic manufacturing
- **Final Outcome**:
	- economically unenforceable, repealed at end of 2nd Jefferson administration replaced by Non-Intercourse act of 1809
		- *Non-Intercourse Act*
			- Lifted Embargos except on Br. or Fr. ports
			- Mostly ineffective again
			- contributed to **War of 1812**
