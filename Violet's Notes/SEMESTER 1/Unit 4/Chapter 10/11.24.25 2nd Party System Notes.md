## Whigs and Democrats (2nd Party System)
- 2nd Party System formed
	- Federalists & Democratic-Republicans -> Dem. Republicans (Era of Good Feelings) -> Whigs (Fed. ideas) & Democrats (Repub. ideas)
	- Rise of **new Political Parties**:
		- Whigs - Henry Clay
		- Democrats - Andrew Jackson
- This time, increase in political participation:
	- Elimination of *property requirements* for voting and <U>universal white, male suffrage</U>
#### Differences Between the parties?
- Role & power of the fed. government (**B.I.T.**)
	- **B**ank of the US
	- **I**nternal improvements (Infrastructure)
	- **T**ariffs
#### Role & Power of the federal government
- Whigs -> favor a strong central government
	- support *Henry Clay's American System*
- Democrats -> favor more power to state gov.
	- Oppose tariffs & American system (farmers oppose tariffs)
#### National Bank
- Democrats -> tend to oppose BUS
	- <U>Jackson's veto of the</U> *3rd BUS* <u>in 1832</u>
- idea of "King Andrew 1st" (**Tyrant King**) -> Whigs created
	- <u>Whigs favored BUS</u>
#### Tariffs
- Tax on imported goods, favored by North & Industries, disliked by South
- *Whigs **support**, Democrats **oppose*** 
- Compromise Tariff of 1833 - lowered rates over 8 years
	- Made by *Henry Clay*
