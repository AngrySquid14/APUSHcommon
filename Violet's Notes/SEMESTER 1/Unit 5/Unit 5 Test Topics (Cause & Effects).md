# Chapter 13
## Expansion Goals/Manifest Destiny
- **Manifest Destiny**
	- based on beliefs in white Americans' *racial & cultural superiority*
	- used to argue it was the U.S.'s "God-given right" to expand Westward
		- Supported by *President Polk* (democrat, expansionist)
- **Expansion Goals**
	- U.S. wants to Manifest Destiny => President Polk wants CA + Southwest 
		- => Texas Border Disputes => *War with Mexico (1846-47)* => Mexican Cession (*Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo* - 1848) => U.S. gains land
			- Gains *California, Nevada, Utah*, + parts of AZ, WY, CO, & NM
## Sectionalism: Slavery Compromises
- **Compromise of 1850**
	- Cause: War w/ Mexico => gain territory => have to decide *if there will be slavery in new territories*
	- Solution: *Henry Clay's 5-prong compromise*
		- California becomes a free state
		- NM & UT decide slavery status by *popular sovereignty*
		- Slave trade ended in DC
		- Congress passes *fugitive slave law*
			- **Fugitive slave act** => <u>any citizen</u> could capture runaway slaves, *even if they were on free soil*
			- => Legal *kidnapping* of Black Americans
			- => Abolitionists become very unpopular, get lynched post-Fugitive Act
		- Texas gives up claims to NM for $10 mil
			- Moderate Southern victory
- **Bleeding Kansas**
	- Cause: *Kansas-Nebraska Act*
		- Act split territory into two areas (Nebraska + Kansas), and let residents decide slavery (*popular sovereignty*)
	- Effect: *Bleeding Kansas*
		- => anti and pro-slavery groups flood Kansas to vote on slavery
			- Violence breaks out, town of Lawrence sacked, leading to the *Pottawatomie Creek Massacre*
				- Massacre done by Abolitionist **John Brown**
		- Sectional divide over slavery grew, and issue led to *the end of the 2nd Party System*
			- Whigs & Democrats divide over slavery => New Republican "**Free-Soil**" party vs. Democrats (Whig party ends.)
		- *Bleeding Kansas also reason for Charles Sumner being assaulted*, => more outrage and sectional divide

## Slavery Arguments: For, Against
- **Northern Arguments**
	- Slavery threatened *economic opportunities*
		- Slavery was making the South a *static & closed society* that upheld an *aristocratic system*
	- **Slave Power Conspiracy** => Slaver would extend, destroying northern capitalism (creating a "Slavocracy")
	- **Free Soil & Free Labor** (Free-soil party)
- **Southern Arguments**
	- Slavery was the "Southern way of life"
	- Agrarian society relied on slavery
	- North was greedy and corrupt
	- Slavery was *justified by religion*
	- *African Americans were not fit to be citizens*

## Secession
- **Immediate cause was the Election of Abraham Lincoln**
	- **Election of 1860**
		- Abraham Lincoln, Stephen Douglass, John Bell, and John Breckinridge all candidates
		- *Outcome*: Lincoln wins election *without a single Southern vote*

	- South outraged, believe election was unfair and illegitimate, southern states secede.
