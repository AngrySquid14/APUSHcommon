## Manifest Destiny
- ### What is It?
	- Term first coined by *John O'Sullivan*
		- Used in July-August 1845 issue of the *Democratic Review*
	- Represented in *American Progress* (*1872*) painting
- ### Significance?
	- Used to argue that it was American's *God-given right* to expand into the West and conquer more land.
		- Argued that America was destined to expand
	- Idea supported by **President James K. Polk** (democrat & expansionist)
## War with Mexico (*1846*-*1847*)
- ### Causes?
	- Manifest Destiny - America wants to expand into West Coast
		- President Polk wanted *California + Southwest*
	- Texas border dispute w/ Mexico @ *Rio Grande*
		- Led to fights between Mexican & American Soldiers
- ### Texas Background
	- *Mexico gained independence from Spain in 1821*
		- Texas wanted slavery, but Mexico banned it in *1829*
	- *1835* - Texas revolts against Mexico, gains independence as *The Republic of Texas*
		- Still has border disputes w/ Mexico
	- *December 1845* - Texas requests to be annexed into U.S. + enter union as slave state
		- Debated in congress b/c *Northerners don't want more territory*, but southerners want Texas to *expand slavery* & cotton production
		- Mexico unhappy with annexation, more tension
- ### U.S. Action
	- President Polk sends Zachary Taylor + 3k troops to *Corpus Christi, TX*, make last diplomatic effort to *solve Texas border issue* AND buy **California & New Mexico** from Mexico
		- Unsuccessful, Polk puts 3k troops in motion, Mexico puts 8k
	- *April 25th, 1846* - Mexican troops attack ~60 of Taylor's soldiers, kill ~12
		- Mexico "**shed American blood upon the American soil**" - Polk
	- *May 13th, 1846* - U.S. declares war, Polk wants New Mexico, California, & more as *compensation*
- ### Course of the War:
	- Battle ground - *California, New Mexico, and Mexico*
	- *March 1847* - **General Winfield Scott** lands 10k troops at Vera Cruz (250k Miles from Mexico City)
		- Captures Vera Cruz, moves to Mexico City
	- *September 1847* - Capital captured, war ends.
- ### Results:
	- 13k Americans killed, 11k+ from disease
	- Cost $100,000,000 (*10x the amount provided by Congress*)
	- **Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo** - *Feb 2nd, 1848*
		- U.S. gains California, Nevada, Utah (parts of AZ, WY, CO, & NM), U.S. paid *$15 million* to Mexico + U.S. govt. pays $3 million debt Mexico owed to American citizen
	- *1853* - **Gadsden Purchase** for $10 Million (Very bottom of AZ & N.M)
		- needed for better terrain to *build a transcontinental railroad to California*
- ### Effects:
	- **Solidified the goal of** ***Manifest Destiny***
	- Caused *increased debates over the expansion of slavery* in newly acquired territory
	- Paved way for *California Gold Rush* & the **American Civil War**
	- Disenfranchised *Mexican & Native inhabitants* in new territory
		- Loose land, political autonomy, etc.
