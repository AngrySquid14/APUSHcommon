## Kansas-Nebraska Act
- Bill authored by Stephen Douglass (Illinois)
	- proposed a bill to create new territory (Railroad) in Midwest to get Southern vote
	- let residents decide slavery (*popular sovereignty*) and split in 2 (*Nebraska & Kansas*)
	- repealed the **Missouri Compromise** as it potentially allowed slavery above 36-30 line
- ### Outcomes 
- #### Bleeding Kansas (~**1856**)
	- Popular Sovereignty => pro and anti-slavery forces flood Kansas to vote
	- Violence breaks out
		- **Lawrence** (a <u>free-state town</u>) sacked
			- Abolitionist **John Brown** + sons retaliate (Pottawatomie Creek Massacre)
	- Issue divided Whigs & Democrats between North & South => new Republican "Free-Soil" party (*End of the 2nd Party System*)
	- **Senator Charles Sumner** verbally attacks **Senator Andrew Pickens Butler** and other Southerners for *supporting bloodshed in Kansas*
		- Senator Butler's nephew **Preston Brooks** travels to senate and beats Sumner unconscious
			- Causes Sumner to leave Senate seat for 3 years
			- Violence supported by South -> more divide as North is outraged
## End of the 2nd Party System
- Divide between Whigs & Democrats => Whig party dissolves
- Federalists -> Whigs -> **Republicans**
- Democratic Republicans -> Democrats -> (still) **Democrats**
- **American (Know Nothing) Party** is formed
	- *Nativist*, anti-immigrant
		- Anti-Irish, Anti-German, and Anti-Chinese(?)
	- Very small party
	- Not focused on slavery debate (hence "Know Nothing")
