## The Emancipation Proclamation (Jan. 1st, 1863)
- Created a *new meaning for the war*
	- War over maintaining Union => War over slavery = **Europe can no longer support south in good conscience**
		- B/C Europe never had slavery (except in colonies)
- Was a war measure to suppress rebellion (Confederacy)
### Terms
- Having slaves becomes a crime
- <u>Slaves are forever free</u>
- Gov. + Military will not hinder former slaves freedom, and will enforce proclamation
- *Military can free slaves in the South*
- **Former slaves can serve in the military**
	- adds **200k black soldiers** to military
### Arguments
- President Lincoln argues act is *necessary for victory*, and is a *just act*
	- Argues it is *warranted by the Constitution*
