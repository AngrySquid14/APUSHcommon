## 1968 Election (Humphrey vs. Nixon)
- ### Democrats' Positions/Ideals?
	- Deeply fragmented, seen as incapable of dealing w/ violence & turmoil caused by Vietnam War
	- Democratic Party candidate -> Vice President *Hubert Humphrey*
	- Advocate for range of "liberal" social issues => extension of civil rights, reproductive rights, fair housing legislation, etc.
- ### Republicans' Positions/Ideals?
	- Oppose Vietnam War
	- Oppose growing federal social programs
	- "converts" => southern Democrats vote Republican in increasing #'s
	- Candidate -> former Vice President *Richard Nixon*
		- Nixon runs on small-govt., anti-war campaign, defender of the **"silent majority"**
		- Advocated for cutting back Fed. power, return power to states
			- known as **"New Federalism"**
### Nixon's Presidency - 1969-1974
- **New Federalism** -> aim to reduce power of fed. govt., make states carry costs of programs (aka Great Society welfare)
- **Environment and Reform** -> Created Environmental Protection Agency, supported Clean Air Act, expanded affirmative action
- **Cold War Policy** -> *Détente*, Improve relations w/ China & Soviet Union, sign SALT
- **Vietnam War** -> Vietnamization, expansion into Cambodia, hate anti-war protests
- **Politics** -> Southern Strategy (capitalize on backlash against civil rights & liberal reforms)
## 1971 - Pentagon Papers
- Secret govt. study of Vietnam War leaked by *Daniel Ellsberg*, military analyst
- <u>Revealed that multiple presidents misled public about Vietnam War</u>
- Published by NYT even though govt. tried to prevent it
	- Supreme Court ruling in *New York Times Co. v. United States* => protected freedom of press (1st Amendment)
- => Increased *distrust* of govt., *"credibility gap"* where what officials told public ≠ reality
## 1972 - Watergate Scandal
- ### What Happened?
	- *1972* -> 5 men caught breaking into the *Democratic National Committee headquarters* @ the Watergate office complex in D.C.
		- Men were linked to Nixon's reelection campaign (*CREEP* - Committee to Re-Elect the President)
	- Break-in bad enough, worse was *Nixon's attempt to cover it up*
		- Nixon secretly taped conversations in Oval Office
	- Long legal battle -> Supreme Court forces Nixon to turn over tapes, revealed that he tried to obstruct justice
- ### Consequences?
	- *Nixon resigned in 1974*, <u>first and only president to do so</u>
	- Led to greater distrust in government
## 1976 - Election (Carter vs. Ford)
- Nixon resigns -> replaced by Vice President *Gerald Ford*
- now-President Ford pardons Nixon as a criminal
- Term ends, Ford runs against Georgia Gov. *Jimmy Carter*
- Carter campaigns as a "Washington Outsider" (Trump...)
	- meaning he wasn't associated with D.C. politics and scandals
	- + Ford's pardon of Nixon hurt his own campaign
- Carter administration struggled with *stagflation* -> stagnant growth, high unemployment, high inflation ("worst of both worlds")
	- Iran Hostage Crisis didn't help
## 1979 - U.S. and the Iranian Revolution
- *1953* -> CIA helps in overthrowing Iran's *elected* leader *Mohammad Mossadegh* -> pro-U.S. Shah installed, *Mohammad Reza Pahlavi* 
	- Shah authoritarian, used secret police to suppress opposition
	- U.S. supported Shah b/c of Cold War (anti-communism, oil access)
- Many Iranians resent U.S. influence, Shah & *Westernization* => *1979* - revolution led by *Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini* overthrew the Shah
	- -> U.S. allowed the exiled Shah into the U.S. => Iranian anger -> *Iran Hostage Crisis*
## Iran Hostage Crisis (1979)
- Post-Iranian Revolution
- Iranian militants seize U.S. embassy in Tehran, take 52 Americans hostage for 444 days
	- Why? Anger over U.S. support of the Shah & him being allowed in the U.S.
- U.S. rescue attempt *failed* -> National embarrassment, <u>Carter's reputation was damaged</u>
- *1981* -> Hostages released & *Ronald Reagan* takes office
## Native American Protests
### Fort Lawton Occupation
- *March 8, 1970* -> ~100 members + sympathizers of the *United Indian People's Council* (**UIPC**) confront 392nd Military Police Company
	- Why? MPC trying to claim part of Fort Lawton (Fort was an army post)
	- Protestors led be *Bob Satiacum* (Puyallup)
		- Read the protestors' proclamation, arguing that native Am. could reclaim the land by *right of discovery*
			- Claim to Ft. Lawton was based on rights given from U.S.-Indian treaties promising return of *surplus military lands*
	- Plan - use Ft. Lawton to create Native Am. studies center, Indian university, ecology center, and a restaurant
- **Results** => Congressional intervention & negotiation -> permitted development of *both* an Indian Cultural Center & Discovery Park
	- *1977* -> Daybreak Star Cultural Center opens, ran by United Indians of All Tribes
### Alcatraz Occupation
- *November 20, 1969* -> *IAT* seizes Alcatraz w/ force of 89 Indians
	- Claim island for all tribes of N.A., graffiti island and move into old prison buildings
		- Also claim Alcatraz with *right of discovery*
- Nixon administration decides to leave IAT alone if they stayed peaceful
- Native Am. students & activists head to island -> Alcatraz pop. grows to 600, govt. council, clinic, public relations department, nursery, and grade school develop.
- *End of 1969* -> Alcatraz pop. ask for contributions, receive 1000s of $, were even visited by celebrities and given a boat
- *Early 1970* -> pop. decline, college students leave and are replaced by vagrants
- *January 1970* -> leader leaves Alcatraz, various activist groups left to fight for control
#### Results/Legacy
- *May 1970* -> Diplomatic efforts fail -> Nixon Admin. cuts all remaining power to Alcatraz
	- -> Fire breaks out across island, destroys several historic buildings
- Few people lived on island for the next year
- *June 11, 1971* -> Occupation officially ends, reason being to restore Alcatraz foghorn & lighthouse
	- 15 people removed by armed federal marshals
	- Occupation lasted *19 months*
- <u>Led to further Indian protests & occupations -> Plymouth Rock, Rushmore, etc.</u>
- *July 1970* -> Nixon calls for Indian self-determination
	- U.S. Govt. returns mil. of acres of Indian land, pass 50+ *legislative proposals supporting tribal self rule*
## Industrial Bust
- U.S. suffering from **Stagflation** and **Deindustrialization**
	- Dismantling of manufacturing affect *Rust Belt* states the most, gain attention 70s-80s
### The Boeing Bust
- *April 16, 1971* -> real-estate agents *Bob McDonald & Jim Youngren* put up billboard near Sea-Tac airport
	- billboard read: *"Will the last person leaving SEATTLE - Turn out the lights."*
	- was put up as response to **The Boeing Bust** 
		- Boeing employees peaked at 100k (1967), then sunk to 39k (1971)
## Oil Crisis (Embargo) - 1973
- *October 17, 1973* ->**The Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries** (OPEC) implements <u>"Oil Diplomacy"</u>
	- Was response to 1973 **Yom Kippur War** between Israel & Palestine
- OPEC places an **Oil Embargo** on Israeli supporters (U.S., Japan, Western Europe)
	- => Gasoline prices in U.S. go from 23 cents/gal. -> 55 cents/gal.
### Effects of Gas Shortage
- Long waits at gas stations
- Gas stations run out of oil -> Gas rationing
- "Self-serve" gas stations become popular
### U.S. Response
- *1974* -> U.S. govt. responds to oil crisis, enacts the *National Maximum Speed* with the **Emergency Highway Energy Conservation Act**
	- NMSL aimed to reduce fuel consumption + improve highway safety
	- <u>Restricted speed limits to 55mph</U>
