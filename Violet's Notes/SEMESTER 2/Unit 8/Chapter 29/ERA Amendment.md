# Era Amendment - 1972
- *March 22, 1972* -> Equal Rights Amendment passed by Congress, sent to states for ratification by both houses
	- Amendment would become part of Constitution if 38/50 (3/4ths) approve
- 1972-1977 -> 35 states approve amendment
- *June 30, 1982* -> Deadline for deciding, no additional states vote yes => ERA Amendment <u>falls only 3 states short of ratification</u>
	- States that didn't ratify:
		- Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Louisiana, Mississippi, Missouri, Nevada, N. Carolina, S. Carolina, Oklahoma, Utah, Virginia
			- Basically Southern (East) states, some Midwest, & Mormon territory
