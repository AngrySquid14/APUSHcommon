# Containment
- Strategy to stop spread of Communism, *not eliminate*
- Proposed by George F. Kennan in 1947
	- U.S. Diplomat living in Moscow
- Was response to Soviet Union's expansion, & belief that *communism spreads in unstable, economically weak regions*
- <u>Was the U.S. strategy in the Cold War</u>
# 2nd Red Scare - 1950s
- ### Senator *Joseph McCarthy* (1904-1957)
	- Elected to Senate in 1946 Wisconsin by *lying about his war service record* & bashing opponents
	- Alcoholic, bad reputation, needed to boost career
	- Tough on communism, America liked this
	- Became powerful for ~4 years, Presidential appointments had to go through him first
	- Charged Rosenberg's (husband & wife) for giving secrets to Soviets (Algier Hiss Trial)
		- Couple was *executed*
- People afraid of communists in America, Soviet spies, etc.
- *How to Spot a Communist* video from U.S. govt. promoted Red Scare & McCarthyism ideas?
# Red Scare Cont.
- 1954 -> McCarthy goes to far, going after U.S. Military & high officials in Pentagon
	- This was stepping on Eisenhower's turf as WWI General/Allied Commander
- Army started investigating McCarthy, not happy w/ efforts to ban books in U.S. administrative libraries
- Media (CBS) turns on McCarthy
- McCarthy dies as an alcoholic, name tarnished
# The Lavender Scare - 1950s
- Moral panic & *political purge* of LGBTQ employees from the U.S. Federal Govt.
	- Happened *parallel to* the Red Scare
- ### Origins
	- Began in 1950 -> Senator McCarthy + others linked "communist subversion" w/ "sexual perversion"
		- Argued that "lavender lads" posed threat to national security
- ### The Purge
	- ~7-10k federal employees fired or resigned
- ### Targeting Tactics
	- Govt. officials conducted intense interrogations, demand names of other individuals
	- Used blackmail to force resignations
- ### Executive Order 10450
	- Signed in 1953 by President Eisenhower
	- Officially included "sexual perversion" as grounds for exclusion from federal employment
# Korean War 1950-1953
- Post WWII, Korea divided at *38th Parallel*
	- Soviet North, U.S. South
- Planned election/ peace settlement never occurred => North & South Korea
- 1950 -> North Korean leader Kim Il Sung invades South Korea to unify peninsula under communism
	- U.S. (under UN) intervened to *contain* communism
- U.S./UN forces pushed North Korean troops close to Chinese border => China entered war, pushed UN forces back to 38th parallel
- Gen. Douglas MacArthur -> want to expand war into China
- President Truman -> refuses to escalate war
# Sputnik 1 - October 4, 1957
- Soviets launch 1st satellite to orbit the earth
	- U.S.S.R. ahead in space race, also put 1st man in space (1961)
- Americans panic, fearing Soviets being able to gather U.S. intel from above
- Crisis prompted President Eisenhower to increase STEM education and found *NASA*
# U-2 Crisis - May 1, 1960
- American U-2 spy plane shot down over *Soviet air space*
	- Pilot taken prisoner
	- U.S. claimed he was doing weather observations
	- Pilot returned a month late in a spy exchange
# Berlin Crisis - June 4-November 9, 1961
- 1000s of young Eastern Germans fleeing to West Berlin monthly -> Soviet Premier (Nikita Khrushchev) had *Berlin Wall* built
	- Wall 26 miles long, concrete w/ barbed, electrified wire
		- + 24-hour watch & massive trenches
	- Wall separated East & West Berlin
# Cuban Missile Crisis
- U.S. Army intel reveals photos showing Soviet-sponsored *nuclear missiles* in Communist *Cuba*
	- -> Missiles were aimed at U.S.
- President Kennedy orders Soviets to remove missiles, surrounds island with *Naval Blockade*
- Lasted 13 days, U.S. & world citizens braced for WWIII
	- Until Soviets *removed the missiles* in exchange for U.S. removing missiles in *Italy & Turkey*
		- U.S. also agreed to never invade Cuba
# Vietnam War - 1955-1975
- U.S. initially involved as military advisor role, supports South Vietnamese (Democratic) regime
- *1965* -> U.S. combat troops arrive
- *1973* -> U.S. combat troops leave
- *1975* -> North Vietnamese troops take Southern capital 
- *1976* -> Saigon renamed Ho Chi Minh
# 1970s
- **Détente** => policy of trying to *ease tensions between the U.S. and the Soviet Union*
- *1972* -> Nixon visits China
- Strategic Arms Limitations Talks (**S.A.L.T**) -> series of agreements between U.S. & U.S.S.R to *limit nuclear warfare*
# The Collapse of the U.S.S.R - December 25, 1991
- *1985* -> Gorbachev makes societal reforms -> "**Perestroika**"
	- => Freedom of:
		- Press/Speech
		- Political Prisoners
		- Religion
		- & Reduced Army control
	- + Policy of "*Glasnost*" (Openness/restructuring Soviet Union)
- *Late 1980s* -> Gorbachev announces he won't interfere w/ independence movements in *Eastern Bloc* countries
- *October 3, 1990* -> Berlin Wall falls => united Eastern & Western Germany
- *Mid 1991* -> Soviet Union ends => <u>ALL former "Eastern Block Nations" are free</u>
