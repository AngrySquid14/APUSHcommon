# Impacts of WWII
 *U.S. in WWII -> Dec 8, 1941 - Sep 2, 1945*
 - Soviet Union faced most destruction from WWII
	 - 1.5 mil civilians & 7.5 mil soldiers killed
- Established U.N. w/ *50 nations*
- *Deciding Power -> Security Council* -> U.S., Br., Fr., Soviet Union, China (Superpowers) 
	- +6 other nations on a rotating basis
- *Potsdam, Germany* July 1945 -> "Big 3" meeting
	- "Big 3" -> U.S., Br., Soviet Union
	- Outcomes:
		- Soviets gain Eastern Europe to use as a *buffer zone* against future attacks
		- Split German capital (Berlin) into East & West
		- Divided Hitler's country into 4 zones, controlled each by U.S.S.R, Britain, France, & U.S.
# "Big 3" Conferences
## Yalta, Ukraine (Feb 4-11, 1945)
- Already decided to divide Germany into occupied zones
- Discussed issue of dealing with defeated + liberated Eastern European nations
- Agreement, accepted by Stalin: have "*interim governmental*" and ASAP establish it through *free elections of govts. by the people*
	- Stalin did not hold up his side of agreement
- UN's charter already drafted, worked out compromise for voting in the Security
## Potsdam, Germany (August 2, 1945)
*note: Roosevelt died April 12. 1945*
- Main issue -> immediate administration of Germany, Poland's borders, & occupying Poland
- Meeting unharmonious, each nation only concerned with own interests
	- Churchill suspicious of Stalin's motives & inflexible position
- Finalized 4 zones of Germany & capital-Berlin, along w/ Austria & capital-Vienna
# Berlin, Germany Zones
- Berlin in East Germany, designated as the Soviet Zone
	- Berlin also divided into 4 zones -> U.S.S.R occupied Eastern half of Berlin
		- -> Many East Berliners escape to West Berlin to avoid Communism
- U.S. -> Want to Reunite Germany, stabilize it & secure Europe
	- -> Rebuild European govts., promote security, provide markets for U.S. goods
	- -> Spread democracy, prevent rise of communist govts.
- U.S.S.R -> Keep Germany divided, preventing it from attacking again
	- -> Rebuild own war-ravaged economy using Eastern Europe's resources
	- -> Spread communism, worldwide workers' revolution
# International Aid & Containment: The Marshall Plan
- Officially the **European Recovery Program**-> U.S. initiative to *Western Europe*
	- Gave $13 Billion to help rebuild economies post-WWII
- Plan operated for 4 years, began in April 1948, had 5 goals
	- 1) Rebuild war-devastated regions
	- 2) remove trade barriers
	- 3) modernize industry
	- 4) make Europe prosperous again
	- 5) prevent/contain spread of communism
- Money divided mainly on per capita basis
	- more money given to major industrial powers, believed to be *essential to overall revival of Europe*
- Soviet counter-plan -> **Molotov Plan** to aid & control *Eastern Europe*
# International Aid: The Truman Doctrine
- 1947-48 -> Multiple events cause Western European nations to become concerned over physical & political security
	- U.S. becomes closely involved in European affairs
- Ongoing *civil war* in Greece, tensions in Turkey => President Harry Truman (**Truman Doctrine**) to provide *economic & military aid* to Turkey + Greece
	- Congress approves $400 Mil. of aid to be sent and to dispatch Am. civilian & military personal + equipment to regions
- Soviet-sponsored coup in *Czechoslovakia* (mid-1948) => Stalin choses to test Western resolve, starts a *blockade* against *West Berlin*
	- blockade ≠ *Berlin Wall*
# Need for "Collective Security" in Western Europe
## NATO
*North Atlantic Treaty (Organization)*
- Signed in 1949
	- After treaty signed, some members requested military aid from U.S.
	- Later in 1949, Truman proposes *military assistance program*, appropriates $1.4 billion for *building Western European defenses*
- Agreement meant that U.S., CA, BE, DK, FR, IS, IT, LU, NL, NO, PT, & UK would see *attack against one = attack against all*
## Collective Security in the East - The Warsaw Pact
- **Warsaw Pact** -> Signed 1955, in Warsaw, Poland
	- Collective defense treaty, established by U.S.S.R, and other Soviet *satellite states* in Central & Eastern Europe
		- Soviet states => Albania, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia, East Germany, Hungary, Poland, Romania
# What is the "Iron Curtain"?
- **Political, Military, & Ideological** barrier dividing *Soviet-controlled* and *Western Democracies*
	- Divide between communism & capitalism (Eastern & Western Bloc)
- *Not* an actual barrier!
# The Berlin Wall
- 26 miles long concrete wall in Berlin, Germany
	- Built by Soviets to prevent people from fleeing to *West Berlin*
- Built in only 3 days
# Domino Theory
- Belief that one country falls to communism => neighboring countries will follow
	- Reasoning led to Korean & Vietnam Wars
