# Nonviolent Resistance
- ### Sit-ins
	- Take up spots in diners(?) stores(?) where black people weren't allowed to be served food
	- Consisted of young people -> would sit all day, weren't arrested often because white people were surprised and confused
- ### Freedom Riders
	- Black & white sometimes
	- Travel into deep south on greyhound buses to protest segregation
		- Often traveling fat, making stops to protest
	- Faced subjugation -> KKK burning buses, severe beatings
	- Riders wouldn't fight back(?)
