- Great Society = high point of *liberalism*
- Similar to New Deal
# LBJ's Great Society
- **Lyndon B. Johnson**
	- Southerner (Texas), humble beginnings
		- Wanted to prove he was compassionate, and to honor JFK (predecessor)
	- Gave election speeches about building a *"Great Society, a place where the meaning of a man's life, matches the marvel of man's labor"*
# Ending Discrimination
- **Civil Rights Act** (1964) -> Ban discrimination in public facilities, schools, employment
	- Originally introduced by JFK
- **Voting Rights Act** (1965) -> End literacy tests, Feds register voters
- **24th Amendment** -> End poll taxes
# The War on Poverty
### Economic Opportunity Act Programs
- **Economic Opportunity Act** -> Several programs for low-income people
	- *Head Start* -> Early childhood education
	- *Job Corps* -> Youth Job training
	- *Upward Bound* -> College Preparation
	- *VISTA* -> Volunteer program, like Peace Corps
	- *CAP* (Community Action Programs) -> Urge communities towards local support
### Other Poverty Programs
- **HUD** (Housing and Urban Development) -> Urban development renewal
- **Elementary and Secondary Education Act** -> $ Billions into Primary & Secondary schools
- **Food Stamp Act** -> Provide food for poor
- **Medicare** -> Extension of social security, provides health care for all Elderly
- **Medicaid** -> Health care program for low income
	- MediCARE = Elderly, MediCAID = all poor
# Environmental & Consumer Protection
- **Clean Air & Clean Water Acts** -> Set standards for air & water quality
- **Highway Safety Act** -> Establish national highway safety program
	- Reduce traffic accidents, deaths, injuries
- **Fair Packaging & Labelling Act** -> Required labels on packaging, protect consumers
	- Packaging required to state what the product is, name & address of manufacturer, & an accurate quantity of the product
# Impacts of the Great Society
### Liberal Ideas and the Court
- **Miranda v. Arizona** -> Criminal Rights ("right to remain silent", etc.)
	- Police required to read citizens' rights
- **Griswold v. Connecticut** -> Right to use contraceptives, right to privacy
