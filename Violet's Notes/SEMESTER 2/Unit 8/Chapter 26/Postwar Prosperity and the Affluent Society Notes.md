# Economic Prosperity
- Economy primed for growth (**demobilization**)
	- Factories producing weapons + military supplies during war -> producing consumer goods (**mechanization**)
	- Increase in goods that weren't available during the war
		- Appliances, TVs, cars become *widely available*
# Growing Private Sector
- **Military-Industrial Complex**
	- Industries that benefited from govt. contracts for military supplies
		- Boeing, General Electric, etc.
	- Soviet launch of Sputnik -> led to the **Space Race** (1957)
		- U.S. passed **National Defense Education Act** (1958) -> increased funding for STEM
# Consumerism Took Root
- Advertisers encouraged families to purchase latest items
	- Played on ideas of status and success
		- . (*cultural phenomenon*)
# Teenagers Targeted
- Teenagers were targeted to buy products by advertisers
- Rise of **teenage rebellion** against post war prosperity
	- Criticized middle-class values + culture
	- Glorified in movies -> Rebel Without a Cause
	- Rise of *Rock n' Roll*
		- Alan Freed place "race" records over Cleveland airways
		- Some of Elvis Presley's songs were covers of black artists 
# The GI Bill
- Key drivers of American "Prosperity"
- Officially known as **The Servicemen's Readjustment Act** (1944)
- Provided returning veterans benefits
	- Low interest rates on home loans
	- Funding for college education
	- Financial support for starting businesses
- Created the *Veterans Administration* (VA)
# Remember Our Veterans
- The U.S. recruited 10s of 1000s of Filipino servicemen to fight w/ it in WWII, *took back promise of benefits*, and citizenship after the war ended
	- Congress passed the **Recission Act** (1946) stripping many Filipino veterans + their families of benefits
- Estimated that ~12-15k Chinese Am. men served
	- Able to obtain education due to G.I. Bill, much like veterans of other ethnic groups
		- Still benefits were sparse????
# Suburban Life
- Suburbia represented *stability & success* -> 25 mil. houses 1945-1970
	- Culmination of the *American Dream*
- Shift to suburban living fueled by the Baby Boom (1945-1964) - Birth control pill
	- Needed more space to accommodate growing families
 ## Levittown
 - 7 Designed, mass-produced suburban communities built by Levitt & Sons
	 - Provide affordable housing for veterans
	 - Long Island / 17k+ homes
	 - Cheap houses, Similar designs
	 - Racial exclusion of black people
## Federal Housing Administration (FHA)
- Provided mortgages w/ little down payment
 - Shelley v. Kraemer (1948) -> illegal to consider race when selling a house
# Suburbia Reinforced Segregation
- Many white Americans moved to the suburbs (called "**White Flight**")
- Chicago, Detroit, New York
	- African Americans moved to larger cities
	- Lived in cities in older buildings, receiving lower paying jobs
	- White Am. took their tax revenue with them
		- Leading to *urban decay*???
