# 1964 Civil Rights Act
- Ended segregation & Jim Crow laws
	- No segregation in Public facilities, employment, businesses, funding, etc.
- However, still voting restrictions due to intimidation or inaccessibility -> very few black voters
# Voting Rights Act of 1965
- Ended racial discrimination in voting
	- -> end of *literacy tests*, poll tax, etc.
- Created *preclearance* (fed. oversight) for changes to voting laws in some jurisdictions
- Had great political affects b/c Southern Black voters increase 
	- Ability to vote for changes w/o racial discrimination
## Post-Voting Rights Act
- Things looking up, but violence still occurring
	- Civil rights leader, James Meredith shot while *peacefully marching*
# Significance of 1968
- Civil Rights movement, Vietnam War, anti-War protests all big at the same time
	- MLK & the SCLC -> the Poor People's Campaign
	- Tet offensive -> North Vietnam
- *June 5, 1968* -> RFK assassinated
- ## MLK Jr.
	- *April 3, 1968* -> MLK Jr. makes **"I have seen the Mountaintop"** speech
		- Speaks on the afterlife "I may not make it (*to the Promised Land*) with you"
	- *April 4, 1968* -> MLK Jr. assassinated
	- *April 4, 1968* (Night) -> Senator RFK makes unscripted speech regarding MLK Jr.'s murder
	- ### Washington D.C. Riots
		- *April 4, 1968* -> unrest leads to riots
			- over 1000 buildings burned, $27 mil in damages
# Civil Rights Leaders
- ### Divisions in Beliefs
	- Carmichael -> part of Black Power Movement, believed in militant action: <u>"no one is asking the southerners to be non-violent"</u>
	- MLK Jr. -> stuck with non-violence, refused for marches to be anything but peaceful
	- *Black leaders disagree, but still work together for common goal*
		- Carmichael & MLK join in "March Against Fear" (June 1966)
- ### Black Power Movement
	- MLK's ideas overtaken by Black Power ideas after *end of segregation*
		- MLK stands on principals of nonviolence
	- Black Power & Black Liberation ideas growing
# Black Power Movement
Late 1960s, Black leaders call for *a different approach to civil rights activism*
- Movement emphasized *racial pride*, economic empowerment, & creation of political & cultural institutions
### Stokley Carmichael
- Founding member of SNCC
- Called for "Black Power" movement in 1966 speech
### Malcolm X
- Black separatist -> only buy from black businesses, self-separation from whites
- Member of Nation of Islam
	- Trip to the Mecca changed opinions on Black separatism, leading to *assassination by NOI*
### Angela Davis
- Not as willing towards violence
- Sought end of *prison-industrial complex*
## Black Panther Party
- Founded in Oakland, CA (1966) by *Booby Seale* & *Huey Newton*
	- Seale marches to CA Capitol protesting gun law -> gain national media attention
- BPP branches formed in other cities
### BPP in Seattle - The Dixon Bros
- Established in 1968 as Seattle charter of BPP
	- one of first charters outside of CA
	- Lasted long, until 1978
- Founding members Aaron & Elmer Dixon
- Grew quickly
- Actions: provide free breakfast, meal delivery, clothes, medicine, etc.
## The 10 Point Program (1966)
### Points (We Want...)
- 1) Freedom & power to determine own community's destiny
- 2) Full employment
- 3) end "to the robbery by the capitalists of our Black Community"
- 4) Decent housing, fit for actual shelter
- 5) Education that teaches true history & true nature of society
- 6) Exempt all Black Men from military service
- 7) End to police brutality & murder of black people
- 8) Freedom for all black incarcerated men
- 9) If brought to trial, black people will be tried by a jury of their peer group (=> black jury)
- 10) Land, bread, housing, education, clothing, justice & peace
### Strategies
- Offer "survival programs" & community services
	- Sponsor schools, legal aid, clothing distribution, transportation, healthcare
- Give real aid to low-income communities, draw support for Panthers
- Advocate for militant resistance against police violence & state oppression
