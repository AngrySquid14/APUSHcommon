## Cleaning Up Urban Environments
- Disease (Cholera, typhoid fever, etc.) spread through cities quickly -> need a clean water initiative
	- Create new sewage + drainage systems in large cities
- "City Beautiful" movement -> goal: increase & improve # of park spaces
### *Tenement Houses*/Dumbbell Tenements
- ~5-6 stories
- cramped apartments -> many families sharing a few rooms
- Dumbbell nickname comes from the building being pinched in the middle, forming a dumbbell shape
- Had tiny light shafts to adhere to an NYC law (1879) requiring windows in every room
	- Tiny shafts gave almost no light
	- Shafts were narrow, could shake neighbor's hand through them
	- Garbage was thrown out of the shafts
		- No way to get into air shaft to clean, so became a fire risk
## Jacob Riis - NYC Photojournalist ("Muckraker"): *How the Other Half Lives*
- aided in exposing suffering poor in tenement houses
- the East Side (still most populated district to this day), exposed as *packed at the rate of 290k people per square mile*
	- Experts testified that rent in the slums were 25-30% higher than rent uptown
- TL;DR Slums rent more expensive than uptown yet also extremely overcrowded
