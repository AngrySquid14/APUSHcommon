# **The Populist Party**
- ## What is it?
	- Political party made up of mostly *farmers*
	- Called for a stronger governmental role in *regulating the American economic system*
		- <u>Want railroad regulation</u> b/c have high rates (price gouging) and farmers had to use them to ship goods
		- Bank and government reform
	- Critical of Capitalism
- ## Populist Party Supporters
	- #### Farmers
		- **The Grange** -> aim to connect farmers, share techniques
			- also aim to elect state legislatures to support programs
		- **Farmers' Alliances** -> mainly local, established stores & banks
	- #### Industrial Workers
			- **Knights of Labor** -> labor union of skilled & unskilled workers
		- *Economic Reformers*
- ## Reasons for Creation 
	- #### Economic Issues
		- Growth of *corporate power*
			- *Railroads* -> high shipping rates hurt farmers
				- RRs often owned grain elevators & charged high storage rates
		- *Panics of 1873 & 1893*
			- Significantly hurt farmers, crop prices plummeted
		- Origins in previous *agrarian* groups
			- Grange
			- Farmers' Alliances
- ## Goals
	- ***Economic relief & government intervention***
	- **Omaha Platform**
		- Direct senator election
		- *Graduated* income tax
		- Secret Australian ballot
	- Government control of railroads, telegraphs, and telephones
	- Free coinage of silver w/ 16:1 ratio w/ gold (**Bimetallism**)
		- 16 oz silver = 1 oz gold
			- Increase money supply -> farmers pay off debts easier (think Shays Rebellion)
			- Supported by farmers and debtors, <U>opposed by Industrialists and lenders</u>
	- *Civil Service Reform*
	- *8-hour workday*
## Election of 1892
- **Grover Cleveland** (Democrat) vs. **Benjamin Harrison** (Republican) vs. **James Weaver** (*Populist*)
	- Weaver campaign on *Omaha platform*
		- Got 22 electoral votes
## Election of 1896
- **William Jennings** (Democrat & *Populist*) vs. **William McKinley** (Republican)
	- Populist party supported Bryan b/c support of Free Silver
	- "Cross of Gold" speech
## Why did the Populist Party Decline
- Rapid economic change
	- more gold discovered
- Farming becomes more mechanized
- Race divided party, esp. in South
- Populists unable to break existing party loyalties
- Democratic Party took over most Populist Goals (co-opted)
## Populists vs. The Government Controlled Economy
- 3 events that populists see as government rigging monetary system *against debtors*
	- establishment of the **National Banking System** in *1863*
	- removal of silver dollars from list of coins to mint in *1873*
	- *repeal* of the **Sherman Silver Purchase Act** in *1893*
- ### Sherman Silver Purchase Act (1890)
	- Passed *in response* to farmers & miners demands for increased money supply to pay off debts
	- Govt. issued treasury notes, can be redeemed for either *silver or gold*
		- -> *investors* bought silver notes, exchanged for gold dollars
			- -> buy more silver, repeat process until *Treasury ran out of gold*
		- Gold depletion contributed to the *Panic of 1893*
			- -> *President Grover Cleveland* repealed act to prevent depletion of Govt. gold reserves
- ## Panics
	- ### Panic of 1873
		- Large unemployment in N. industry
		- response was labor strikes
	- ### Panic of 1893
		- 2.5 mil & 20% unemployment rate
		- Pres. Cleveland responds with hands-off (laissez-faire) approach, champions *gold standard*
	- Both panics caused by *overspeculation, overbuilding, & deflation*
		- deflation caused by lack of money supply
			- gold standard => less silver => lack paper $
