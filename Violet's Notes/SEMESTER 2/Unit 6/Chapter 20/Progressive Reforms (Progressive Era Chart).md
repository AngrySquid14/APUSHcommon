## Roosevelt's Square Deal
- Based on 3 basic ideas: 3 C's
	- *Conservation* of Natural Resources
	- *Control* Corporations
	- *Consumer* Protection
- Issue => **Conservation, Economy**
## Hepburn Act (1906)
- Enabled I.C.C. (Interstate Commerce Commission) to *set shipping rates*
	- part of controlling *large-scale enterprise*/trusts
- Issue => **Economy**
## National Consumers' League (NCL)
- Started as New York Consumers' League, founded in 1890 with help of *Josephine Shaw Lowell*
	- Organization aimed to improve conditions for female clerks in NY
- Encouraged consumers to only purchase from *white list* stores
	- White list => stores known to have fair wages & working conditions
- NCL headed by *Florence Kelley*
	- Advocated for *protective legislation*
- Issue => **Social Justice**
### Muller v. Oregon
- Supreme Court ruled in favor of Oregon law limiting workday to 10 hours for women
	- *Pros*: Ruling encouraged women's organizations to lobby for more reforms -> first law giving public assistance to single mothers, minimum wage law for women, Children's Bureau -> Women's Bureau
	- *Cons*: Muller case *did not protect men*; Brandeis' case rested on women being potential mothers, who had to be protected *for sake of future children* -> women protected because motherhood, men still overworked
		- led to divisions between women's rights advocates and labor advocates
- NCL worked to win case and succeeded, hired *Louis Brandeis* as lawyer
	- Brandeis evidence that women's health suffered from long work hours would lead to use of *social-science* research in legal decisions
- Issue => **Social Justice**
## W.E.B. Du Bois
- #### Niagara Movement
	- meeting on Canadian side of Niagara falls
		- Du Bois + William Trotter created meeting
	- Principles
		- full voting rights, *end to segregation*, equal treatment in justice system, equal opportunity in education, jobs, health care, etc. (*Full equality*)
		- Principles would guide future *Civil Rights Movement*
- #### NAACP
	- founded by *Mary Ovington*, sympathetic progressive group
	- Du Bois later joined as editor for NAACP journal
- Issue => **Social Justice**
## Robert La Follette
- Republican governor (1901-1905)
- Principles
	- promoted *Wisconsin Idea* => greater government intervention in *economy*
	- relied on UW economists for policy recommendations
	- had strong commitment to *democracy*
	- advocated to protect workers & *restrict corporate power*
- Achievements
	- restricted lobbying
	- gave Wisconsin citizens the **right of recall** => ability to vote to remove unpopular politicians from office
	- gave Wisconsin citizens **referendum** => vote *directly on a proposed policy*, <u>rather than leaving it to elected legislators</u>
- Issues => **Economy, Democracy**
## Wobblies Syndicalists
- Syndicalists -> believed that resisting and throwing a *general strike* in workplace would lead to workers overthrowing capitalism
	- Believed new society would emerge, ran by workers
