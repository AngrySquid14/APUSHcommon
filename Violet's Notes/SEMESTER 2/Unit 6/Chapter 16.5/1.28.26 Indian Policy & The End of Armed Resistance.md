## Native Point of View/Perspectives
- Saw *reformers* as "just another interest group"
	- Surrounded by white people giving multiple messages
- Chiefs confused b/c many interest groups made dealing with U.S. hard
	- Some believed whites had too many chiefs/leaders
	- Contracts made in person would often later be denied or altered by Congress or Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA)
- Confusion led to distain for American leaders
## Government (Congress and Supreme Court) Decisions:
- **1830s** - Supreme Court declares Indians <u>no longer sovereign</u> -> tribes become *domestic independent nations*
	- => *independent* tribal governance and treaty-making supposed to end
		- Still, treaties negotiated as late as *1869*
- **1871** - House of Representatives passes bill ending *all* treaty making with Indians
	- Senate agrees to bill *if* remaining treaties still function
- Government decisions worked to <u>erode native rights</u>
## 1887 Dawes Severalty Act
- ### Intentions
	- part of reformers' goal to assimilate Natives
	- *Henry L. Dawes* (Massachusetts) -> Hoped to end reservation system and give Natives **severalty** (individual ownership of land)
		- How? Divide reservations -> homesteads (like white farmers)
- Plan supported b/c people thought it would encourage Natives to assimilate
- Some *don't support plan*, b/c believed <u>property ownership would make Natives greedy</u>
- ### Actual Outcome
	- **Dawes Severalty act ***failed tremendously***
		- Whites wanting Indian lands => convinced people that Indian lands *not being reallocated* be sold to non-Indians
		- BIA => implements law with little regard => government commission seizes >15 million acres of land from *Indian Territory* by *1894*
			- Stolen land => formed into Oklahoma
		- Before act, Natives held >*155* mil. acres, drops to *77* mil. acres
		- Natives pressured to sell land
	- TL;DR: Main causes of lost land = BIA mismanagement, fraud, and pressuring Natives to sell land
## Battle of Little Bighorn/Custer's Last Stand
- **1874** - Lakota provoked b/c *General **George Armstrong Custer*** leads expedition into S. Dakota's Black Hills & discovers gold
	- => Prospectors rush to Black Hills => Sioux (Lakota) Chiefs pressured to sell Black Hills by U.S.
		- => <u>Chiefs say no</u>, Gov. *demands* all Sioux gather @ federal agencies
