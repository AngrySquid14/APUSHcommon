
**Note: Following victory + Treaty of Paris, British give up *Appalachian Western Territory*, U.S. Gov viewed Br. Indian Allies as conquered peoples, assert authority & ownership over the region**
- *Tribes rejected this notion, did not sign or take place in Treaty of Paris*
**Native Americans made *tribal alliances* (Western Confederation, Creek Confederacy)**
- Native Am. fight Am. military, attack intruders
- Am. negotiators use *bribes, threats, and **alcohol*** to get tribal member to give up land in Western Pennsylvania and New York (Treaty of Fort Stanwix)
	- Similar tactics used to get Ohio land
		- In Ohio, Tribal leaders disavow agreements, as they were illegitimate & coerced
- Ohio tribes join with *Western Confederacy*, crush military forces from Washington in 1790 & 1791
	- *Concerns that Confederation would ally w/ British caused Washington to double military strength in Ohio*
### Battle of Fallen Timbers *(1794)*
- U.S. won, defeating Western Confederacy
- Fought in Toledo, Ohio
- Named after the trees that fell during the battle
### Treaty of Greenville *(1795)*
- Tribes still resistant after Battle of Fallen Timbers, U.S. has to compromise
- Treaty did/said:
	- acknowledged Indian land 
	- Recognized many Indian rights; treaty was negotiated as *relative equals* on battlefield
- Treaty caused British to agree to reduce trade & military aid to tribes in the region
### How did the U.S acquire Native American lands with such ease?
***THE CONSTITUTION PROVIDED NO GUIDANCE FOR AMERICAN INDIAN TRIBES AND FEDERAL GOVERNMENT RELATIONS***
- Gov. used threat of military action to force treaties on Native Am.
- Bribed Native Am. to use American goods (to compel them to give up lands)
- Gov. allowed settlers to intrude on Native Am. lands, force issue of land ownership
- Gov. policy - deal w/ 1 tribe at a time, keeping them divided
- Other efforts:
	- Gov. encourage Natives to assimilate into white society
	- Use missionaries to promote Christianity + American beliefs
	- Native Am. made tribal alliances (NW Confederation, Creek Confederacy)
	- Native Am. fought Am. Military, make attacks on intruders
### The Northwest Ordinance *(1787)*
- Ordinance stated that Indians were to be treated w/ **"upmost good faith"** and specified that *"their lands and property shall never be taken away from them without their consent"*
- However, as settlers pushed into occupied Indian territory, they received military protection
- Gov. of Indiana William Henry Harrison threatened, bribed, purposely intoxicated Indians
- 1811 & 1812, Harrison fights and defeats Tecumseh at battle of Tippecanoe.
