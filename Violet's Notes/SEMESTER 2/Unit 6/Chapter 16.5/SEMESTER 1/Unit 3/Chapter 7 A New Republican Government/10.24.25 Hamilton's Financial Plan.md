#### Early Republic's Financial Problems
- $80 Million+ in debt
	- Foreign Debt: $11.7 Mil
	- State Debt: $21.5 Mil
	- Federal Domestic Debt: $42.4 Mil
#### Hamilton's Economic Plan
- *Stabilize the Banking System*
	- Create a **National Bank
	- Create a **National Mint**
- Take over most of the states' *$25 million* Revolutionary War debt
	- Give states a clean slate, free up state money for business & trade
	- One large national debt = ~$76 million
	- Restores faith in public credit, *shows strength in national government*
- *Gain Revenue*
	- Pass **tariff** to bring in $ & help American manufacturers
		- Imports get tariff tax, which goes to US Treasury
		- Promotes American goods over foreign imports
		- ***WAS in Hamilton's Plan, DID NOT make the final cut!!!***
	- Sell **National Bonds**
		- Invest money in the US, investor will be paid back w/ interest
		- US Bond = Cash into US Treasury
	- **Excise Tax/Whiskey Tax** (Sin Tax)
		- National tax on luxury items and Whiskey
	- **Bank of the United States**
		- Print Paper $
		- Collect Tax revenue
		- Pay interest on Debt
		- Offer commercial loans for new businesses/industry
#### BUS Controversy (Jefferson vs. Hamilton)
- **Is the Bank of the U.S. Constitutional?
	- Jefferson: Constitution list of Legislative Powers *never specifically says* the legislature can create a bank
	- Hamilton: Article I, Section 8 says "make all laws which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into execution the foregoing powers, and all other powers vested by this Constitution."
	- Jefferson strict constructionist, Hamilton loose constructionist
		- Strict interpretation of Constitution vs Loose interpretation
	- **Issues of Hamilton's Plan will foster grown of *Political Parties***
		- Hamilton's plan promotes Industry & Manufacturing vs. Jeffersons' *Agrarian* vision for US
##### Alexander Hamilton: Secretary of the Treasury
- Wanted Bank of the US (BUS)
- Wanted shift from Agriculture to Manufacturing
#### Getting the Economic Plan Accepted
- **Hamilton's Compromise
	- South agreed to allow federal government to take debts of northern states
	- North agreed to move US Capital to South (Washington, DC)
		- Capital was in Philly for a bit
