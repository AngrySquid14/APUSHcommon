- Most population -> *North* (2/3rds)
- Bigger industry + better transportation -> *North*
- More resources -> *North*
- Better military leaders -> *South*
- Willingness to fight & win war -> *South*

## Confederate War Strategies
- Fight a **Defensive War**
	- Conserve resources, don't risk as man soldiers
- Become recognized by Britain and France
	- need for cotton trade
## Union War Strategies
- **Anaconda Plan**
	- Blockade Southern Ports, stopping trade and flow of supplies
	- Made by *General Winfield Scott*
