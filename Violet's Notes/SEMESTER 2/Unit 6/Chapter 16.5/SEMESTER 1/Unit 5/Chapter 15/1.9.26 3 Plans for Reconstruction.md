## Lincoln
***Goal was to reunify Union***
- More lenient approach to bringing Southern states back into Union
- **10 Percent Plan**
	- if 10% of voters in Confederate state swear loyalty, can rejoin union
		- also have to accept 13th Amendment
	- Plan was rejected by Confederate states
- Former Confederate leaders excluded from pardons
- *Didn't clearly define rights for formerly enslaved*
