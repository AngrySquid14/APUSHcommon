### Missouri Compromise
- Congressional debate on how to keep slave/free balance
	- Made to avoid large divide over slavery
- 36-30 line (States north of it are free, south of it are slave)
	- Slave states and free states have to be added alternatively to keep balance
	- Missouri becomes slave state
	- Maine made as free state
- Lasted ~30 years, was a Band-Aid solution

### Political Parties
- Whigs - federalist ideas, oppose Andrew Jackson
	- Wealthy, Northern, urban
	- want internal improvements & tariffs
		- To protect domestic manufacturing
	- dislike Jackson b/c his vetoing of BUS
		- "King Andrew the 1st"
	- Name inspired by England's "glorious revolution"
- Democrats - democratic republican ideas, oppose BUS, internal improvements
	- Rural, southern and midwestern?
	- Anti-tariff
		- b/c tariffs raised prices of goods
	- Name inspired by Democracy - support common man
- Universal white male suffrage starts
### The American System
- Purpose: to further develop/boost America's infrastructure and economy
	- Many areas undeveloped
- Components:
	- Internal improvements - Federal gov. funds construction of railroads, canals, roads, etc.
	- Tariffs - made to protect domestic manufacturing/build national economy
	- 2nd BUS - to build national economy
		- got vetoed
### Monroe Doctrine
- Decision under President Monroe that the U.S. would stay out of European affairs
	- policy of Neutrality (Origin: George Washington's farewell address)
	- In turn, Europe would have to stay out of Latin America
- Results? U.S. became dominant power in Western Hemisphere
- Why? Post-War of 1812 - U.S. more nationalistic after defeating Britain
