- Josiah Priest - *Bible Defense of Slavery* (1851) (outside of this unit!!)
- **Slavery Arguments**

### Antislavery Efforts/Arguments
- Southern antislavery efforts -> failed rebellions by enslaved people
- African Americans create communities to preserve culture and family structure
- 19th century continues, *antislavery efforts increase in the north*
- **David Walker** (*Black*)
	- Created *An Appeal to the Colored Citizens of the World*
		- Used *Bible* and *Declaration of Independence* in writings
		- <u>critiqued colonization</u> (moving African Americans to *Liberia*)
- **Nat Turner**
	- "Christ is an avenging God of justice..."
	- Led *one of the largest slave rebellions in history* (**1831**)
		- Caused slave codes to become stricter afterwards
- **William Lloyd Garrison**
	- Creator of *The Liberator* (1831-1865, Boston)
		- Newspaper that called for **immediate & uncompensated end of slavery**
		- <u>against colonization</u>
		- criticized Constitution b/c it was complicit w/ slavery
- **Frederick Douglass** (*Black*)
	- Former slave from Maryland
	- Orator, staunch abolitionist & women's rights supporter
	- Created abolition newspaper (North Star)
	- "What, to the American slave, is your 4th of July?"
- **American Anti-Slavery Society** (*1833-1870*)
	- 250k members nationwide
	- Several African Americans on board of directors
		- Theodore S. Wright - co founder
			- Used *religious arguments against slavery*
			- <u>Conductor on the Underground Railroad</U>
- **Theodore Weld**
	- Minister, preached in North about evils of slavery
		- Believed slavery was a sin
	- *Important in shaping movement from gradual emancipation -> immediate emancipation*
### Slavery Expansion
- No more slave trade (banned in *1808*)
	- related to gradual emancipation?
	- <u>Means slave trade is *NOT* a cause of slavery expansion!!!</u>
- Overcultivation of land b/c of cotton farming -> plantation owners seek out land <u>west of Appalachians</u>
	- Plantation owners move west -> slavery institution and slaves move west
- *Deep South/"Cotton Belt"* - MS, AL, LA
### Colonization
- *prior to 1830*, many politicians support colonization efforts of former slaves in Africa
	- Henry Clay, John Marshall, Daniel Webster
- **1816** - American Colonization Society founded
	- Called for gradual emancipation AND moving black people to Africa
		- *Liberia* established on West Coast of Africa
