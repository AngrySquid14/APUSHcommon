### "The Era of Good Feelings"
#### Nationalism Spirit
- reflected in *expanded economy*, growth of *white settlement + trade in the West*, and the creation of *new states*
- Nationalism growing after **War of 1812**
#### End of 1st Party System
- **Decline of Federalists (little opposition)**
	- Monroe reelected w/o opposition in 1820, Federalist party ceases to exist
	- *Conclusion of War of 1812 and dream (political)*
		- Nation faced no international threats after war
		- Am. Politicians dream of the end of *partisan divisions* & *factional disputes*
			- Monroe attempts to use office to made dream come true
	- *Monroe appointments*
		James Monroe, appointed by James Madison, appointed by Thomas Jefferson
		- Monroe appoints (former Federalist + NE) John Quincy Adams as <u>Secretary of State</u>
			- Suggests end to *"Virginia Dynasty"* in presidency, as Jefferson, Madison, + Monroe were all SOS before being Pres.
		- Monroe appoints John C. Calhoun as <U>Secretary of War</u> after Henry Clay (Speaker of the House) declines
		<U>Monroe takes care to appoint Northerners, Southerners, Easterners, Westerners, Federalists, and Republicans</U> (*political dream*)
	- *Goodwill tour, Boston Newspaper*
		- President Monroe does goodwill tour throughout country
			- Not done by any president since Washington
			- Greeted everywhere throughout country
		- Boston Newspaper, The *Columbian Centinel* (Federalist)
			- commented on *"Presidential Jubilee* in the city, and observed an *"era of good feelings"*
