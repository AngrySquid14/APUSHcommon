## Toward a Democratic Republican Culture
### Changing Family Dynamics
- Mercy Otis Warren - argued against patriarchy (male family dominance)
#### What ways did views toward marriage change?
- Marriage for love, not for political/economic gain
	- **Companionate marriages**
	- Marry for feelings, not *status*
- Husbands had more power still
#### What ways did family size *and* childrearing change?
##### Family Size
- 8-9 members in family in 1750s, down to ~6 in 1810
	- caused by Less single men, delayed marriages, birth control
- Less children = easier to provide for (father), and less time spent bearing and raising children (mother)
##### Childrearing
- **Republican Motherhood**
	- Mothers raise children to be good citizens
	- Mothers take active role in education
		- This idea made education more accessible to women
##### Raising Republican Children
- *Primogeniture* abandoned by most states, requiring a man's estate to be divide among *all* children if he died w/o a will
- Two modes of parenting:
	- Encouraging independence v. authoritarianism (middle/upper class enlightened - )
##### Debates over Education?
- Jefferson - advocate education for Americans
	- Educated voters
- More schools in the North, *Why?*
	- Denser population
	- Southern plantation owners focus on hired tutors for own children, rather than building schools.
## Aristocratic Republicanism and Slavery
#### Impact of the Revolution on slavery?
- Wartime loyalty allowed slaves to be freed in Maryland and Virginia
- Manumission and Gradual Emancipation:
	- *Manumission*: freeing of slaves by owners. VA Manumission Act (1782) owners freed 10k slaves
	- Quakers + enlightenment thought challenged slavery
	- Free black people significantly discriminated against
#### Defense of slavery?
- VA legislature passed new manumission law in 1792 banning freedom (w/ Rev. war, loyalty freed 10k)
- Slavery first defended as a *"necessary evil"* to maintain white supremacy + luxurious planter lifestyle (Thomas Jefferson)
- 1800 - Gabriel Prosser planned slavery uprising, him + 30 others hanged. "Liberty and equality have brought the evil upon us" -*Virginia Herald*
	- led to liberty and equality of white's **Herrenvolk** (master race) **Republic**
#### Emphasis on education?
- North emphasize education, creating significantly higher literacy rates compared to South
#### Slavery and National Politics:
- 1800, northern states have laws ending slavery
- South sees slavery as a *"positive good"* now
	- Southern dominance in federal government protects slavery
#### African Americans Speak Out:
- Slavery increased w/ **cotton boom** (cotton gin)
- *American Colonization Society*
	- Proposed free slaves would be sent to Africa (Liberia)
		- many African Am. rejected idea
- New black churches developed
