## The Sioux Alliance
- **formed in the *1600s*** 
- Late *1800s* -> incr. in soldiers, miners + fort construction in territory (WI, MN, Dakotas, etc.) = major threat to the Lakota's existence
	- Leaders of 4 Lakota tribes meet to reassert alliance & mutual protection
		- *Red Cloud* -> strategist
		- *Crazy Horse* -> warrior
		- *Sitting Bull* -> revered spokesman + holy man
		- *Gall* -> ally to Sitting Bull, fearless leader
	- *Alliance was crucial to the Lakota's survival + fight against encroachment of* <u>white settlers, miners, + U.S. Army</u>
- ### The Great Sioux Wars - Native Strategy
	- Strategy was to stretch out Army, then *attack forts*
		- Army wasn't big enough to safeguard forts and against assaults on trail => Sioux attack settlers moving west
	- *Attack, Retreat, Ambush*
- ### Battle of Little Bighorn (*Montana*) - 1876
	- Miners invade <u>Sioux Reservation</u> in the *Black Hills*
		- -> Sitting Bull + Crazy Horse form (large) force to drive miners out
	- Custer leads U.S. Army into Little Big Horn River, where his * ~250 troops are slaughtered by Sitting Bull + Crazy Horse's forces*
		- Army force became outnumbered & are *abandoned by Custer & his troops*, try to retreat but <u>fall into tribal village</u>(?) and are overwhelmed
			- Custer's force outnumbered 4 to 1 -> end up running up hill -> slaughtered by Native forces
	- ### Ghost Dance
		- After battle, tribal groups perform a **Ghost Dance** ritual
			- Army becomes suspicious of ritual, arrest Sitting Bull
				- -> Sitting Bull murdered in captivity
		- At **Wounded Knee** => 100+ men, women, & children <u>slaughtered by U.S. Cavalry</u>, *marking the end of **Native American Armed Resistance***
## Chief Joseph + The Nez Perce Tribe (Idaho) - 1877
- **The Nez Perce Tribe** of *Idaho*, led by **Chief Joseph**
	- Tribe was to be *removed* and sent to *Oklahoma* (Indian Territory)
	- => Chief Joseph escapes to Canada -> Caught at border - "I will fight no more forever" -> Chief Joseph returned to Oklahoma
- ***Last remaining Native group to attempt to resist***
## 1887 Dawes Act
- Was a response to American outcries of "wretched" Native Am. situation
	- Act provided **160 acres** to tribal families to turn into a *self-sufficient family farm* & to assimilate into American culture
		- Act was ignorant to Native values & caused damage to tribal culture and lives
- *only Native Americans who accepted 160 acres could become citizens*
## End of The Great Sioux Reservation
- **The Great Sioux Reservation** -> established by Fort Laramie Treaty of 1868, included <u>all of western South Dakota and Northern Nebraska</u>
	- -> Dawes Act broke up communal Indian lands into *individual family holdings*
	- 1889, Congress passes another act before N. + S. Dakota joined Union
		- <u>Act partitioned Great Sioux Reservation into 5 smaller reservations</u>
			- Standing Rock, Cheyenne River, Lower Brule, Rosebud, Pine Ridge
		- Act left out 9 mil. acres (HALF of the Great Sioux Reservation) for public purchase
			- Most of area not homesteaded until 1900s
