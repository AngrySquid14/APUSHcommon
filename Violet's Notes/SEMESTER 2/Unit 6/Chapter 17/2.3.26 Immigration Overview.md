# Asian Immigration
## Chinese
- 1850s - original large immigration b/c of *California Gold Rush*
- Immigration reasons
	- Famine in China
	- Lack of jobs
	- Overpopulation
	- U.S. needed railroad, mines, factory & farm workers
- Chinese relegated to **non-threatening** jobs due to white resentment
	- Cooks, garment makers, laundry men, domestic work
- *1882*: **Chinese Exclusion Act** -> stops Chinese immigration to America
	- Women and families can't enter U.S.
## Japanese
- First came to Hawaii in 1868 to work on *sugar plantations*
- Immigration reasons
	- Lack of land
	- Over crowding
	- Many immigrants were 2nd, 3rd, 4th sons who were not going to inherit land
- Many Japanese immigrate as laborers because of *Chinese Exclusion Act*
- Many men single, gain wives back home through "*Picture Bride*" system
- Become successful in agriculture because of ability to work with limited space
## Filipinos
- 1st group arrives after Philippines *becomes territory of U.S.* -> most go to Hawaii
- Filipinos sought out *AFTER* Japanese immigration is also restricted (1910s)
- Discriminated against, but <u>Exclusion Act could NOT be passed against Filipinos because the Philippines was a territory of the U.S.</u>
	- Couldn't be classified as *aliens*
- **Way after this unit btw** -> Filipinos restricted in 1930's after Philippines gets independence
## Koreans
- First arrived in 1903 b/c of *drought*, also came to Hawaii, then U.S.
- 1905 Russo-Japanese War -> Japanese invasion halted Korean immigration
- 1945-1960 -> **Korean War** -> legislation brings war refugees, orphans, and Korean women *married to U.S. soldiers*
## Southeast Asia: Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos
- 1970s - After the **Vietnam War** & **Communist Takeovers**, many immigrate as war refugees facing retaliation for supporting U.S. and not communism 
# Latin Immigration
## Mexicans
- Late 1800s - New land law in Mexico allows rich people in Mexico to gain large amounts of land -> Mexican farm workers work for rich and new landowners
	- New owners did not allow profits to go to workers
- Loss of land and bad wages -> Mexicans go to work in U.S., replacing Chinese workers in same jobs (railroads, mining, etc.)
## Puerto Ricans and Cubans
- Moved to U.S. from 1870-1898 escaping war & Spanish Gov.
- Many well educated, move to New York + N.E. cities to continue professions
- Poor Cubans move to Florida -> become cigar makers
# European Immigration
## Old Immigration (1820s-1880s) Western Europe
- Motives
	- Potato famine -> Irish immigration
	- Political turmoil -> German immigration
- Irish poor, settle in *Eastern cities* as laborers
- Germans have some $, settle *West* as farmers
## New Immigration (1890-1920) South & Eastern Europe
- Motives
	- Rich bought up small farms, machines took jobs -> Italian and Greek immigration
	- Hatred and prejudice -> Jewish (Polish & Russian) immigration
	- Economic and Political trouble -> Scandinavian (Norway, Swedish, Danish, Finnish) immigration
- Italian & Greek become laborers in *Northeast*
- Polish & Russian Jews settle in *Northeast* 
- Scandinavians settle in the *Great Lakes* region as farmers and fishers
