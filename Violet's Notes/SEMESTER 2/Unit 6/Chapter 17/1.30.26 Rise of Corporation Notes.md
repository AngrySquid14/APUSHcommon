# Vocab
### Predatory Pricing
- Business practice where company temporarily lowers prices *below cost* to drive competitors out of the market
	- Company can then *raise prices* once competition is eliminated
		- Means company controls the price of good
### Horizontal Integration
- Take market shares + control prices by *absorbing rival firms*
	- Either by banding companies together (cooperation) or *hostile takeover*
### Vertical Integration
- Control of production via acquiring ownership of <u>ALL parts of the manufacturing process</u>
	- Ex. a Steel Company owning the coal + iron mines as well as the rail lines & factory
	- Ex. Amazon
### Mass Production
- Factory system
	- Combines advanced machinery w/ a disciplined labor force & assembly line
	- Produces large amounts of *identical goods* at a <u>low cost</u> 
		- Ex. Textiles & Meat-Packing
### "Deskilling"
- Part of Mass Production
- Replaces *skilled workers* (hand-made production) w/ *unskilled low-wage workers* on assembly lines
	- Included women and *children* being paid very little
# Trust vs. Monopolies
## Trust
- *Multiple companies* in the <u>same industry</U> under control of one *board/group*
	- Companies may still exist as separate entities
- **Goal**: *Reduce* competition and <u>control prices</u>
## Monopolies
- *one dominant company* in an industry, single firm <u>controls market</u>
- **Goal**: *Eliminate* competition, control prices <u>and supply</u>
- Many trusts become monopolies b/c trusts reduce competition, making it easier for a group to dominate an industry
