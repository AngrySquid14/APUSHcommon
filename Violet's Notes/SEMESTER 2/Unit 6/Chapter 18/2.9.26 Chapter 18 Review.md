# Commerce and Culture
- ## Consumer Spaces
	- Businesses seek to attract *middle-class women*
		- P.T. Barnum's Circus - family appeal
		- Department stores, store credit
	- Electricity -> *new appliances*
		- Washing machines, vacuum cleaners
	- Pullman Palace Cars
		- Luxury train cars
	- *Plessy v. Ferguson* (1896) upheld segregation
		- "separate but equal"
		- upheld legality of *Jim Crow Laws*
		- Case overturned in 1954 (*Brown v. Board of Education*)
- ## Masculinity and the Rise of Sports
	- **"Muscular Christianity"**
		- YMCA - Appeal to professionals and working-class men
		- Promoted *Christianity and fitness*
	- **America's Game**
		- Baseball becomes popular in 1870s
			- Provided Americans w/ new leisure activity
			- First professional baseball team - Cincinnati Red Stockings
	- **Rise of the Negro Leagues**
		- Black people excluded from baseball -> Negro Leagues formed
	- **American Football**
		- Collegiate football becomes popular under Yale's Walter Camp, which utilized *Scientific Management*
- ## The Great Outdoors
	- *Sierra Club* - Sought to preserve American mountains
	- *Yellowstone* (WY) created in 1872
	- National Park Service created in 1916
	- *Antiquities Act* (1906) -> president could set aside objects as *national monuments*
	- States pass laws regulating hunting + fishing to preserve wildlife
- ## Changes in Family Life
	- Childbearing *decreased drastically*
		- b/c marriage later in life + contraceptives becoming widespread
	- *Comstock Act* (1873) -> *prohibited* distribution of info about sex + birth control
- ## Education
	- over 70% of Americans ages 5-18 attend school *by 1900*
	- **Tuskegee Institute**
		- Founded by *Booker T. Washington* (former slave)
		- Advocated for black people to learn *industrial education or vocational training*
	- **Atlanta Compromise**
		- Washington argued for AA to have *equal economic opportunities*
	- *Women's education opportunities **increased** post-Civil War* 
		- Many women attend Black Colleges (created during reconstruction)
- ## From Domesticity to Women's Rights
	- **Maternalism** 
		- Justified women's involvement in *reform movements*
	- **The Woman's Christian Temperance Union*
		- 1874, *Frances Willard*
		- Sought to challenge *alcohol + domestic violence*
		- Key to the **Prohibition Movement**
			- Movement divided along religious, ethnic, and class lines
	- **Women, Race, and Patriotism**
		- United Daughters of the Confederacy (**UDC**)
			- Create monuments, give out Confederate flags, etc.
			- *reshaped the image of the Civil War*
		- National Association of Colored Women (**NACW**)
			- Reform organization
				- Focused on orphans, elderly, those in need of homes
				- Advocated temperance
		- **Ida B. Wells**
			- Journalist, wrote about the horrors of lynching
			- Demonstrated lynchings were often result of labor + economic disputes or relationships between black men & white women
	- **Women's Rights**
		- **NAWSA** - National American Woman Suffrage Association (1890)
			- Many western states provide women's suffrage (WY, CO, ID, UT)
			- 1913 -> many women *west of Mississippi River* granted suffrage
		- **Feminism*** 
			- Advocate political, economic, and social equality for women
- ## Darwinism and Its Critics
	- **Herbert Spencer** and "*Social Darwinism*" 
		- "survival of the fittest"
		- Used by wealthy to *justify* their wealth
		- Success and wealth => wealthy were "naturally selected"
	- **Eugenics**
		- Practice of preventing certain individuals from reproducing
- ## Realism in the Arts
	- **Realism**
		- Representing life in *realistic terms*
	- **Mark Twain**
		- Coined term "Gilded Age"
		- *Anti-Imperialist*, critiqued American progress
	- Many realism + modernism artists were men
- ## Religion: Diversity and Innovation
	- **Immigrant Faiths**
		- Catholic + Jewish immigrants
			- struggle to keep traditions vs. becoming "American"
		- Italians + Polish establish own parishes and schools
	- **Protestant Innovations
		- American Protective Association (*APA*)
			- Similar to the *Know-Nothing Party* (1840s-1850s)
			- Sought to **ban** *Catholics* from holding office
		- **Social Gospel**
			- Protestant Church movement, sought to improve society vis ending injustice & *improving social welfare*
				- Reading rooms, nurseries, etc. provided to the needy
		- *Salvation Army*
			- Helped establish soup kitchens and shelters
		- *Fundamentalism*
			- Focused on a *strict and literal* interpretation of the Bible
			- Will play important role in 1920s
