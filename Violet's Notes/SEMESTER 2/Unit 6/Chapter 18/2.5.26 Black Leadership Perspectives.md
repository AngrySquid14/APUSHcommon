# Black Leadership Perspectives
## Booker T. Washington
- Most prominent African American leader of the time
- Former slave
- ### Strategies + Goals to improve Black lives?
	- Founded the Tuskegee Institute in 1881
	- Goal was **self-help** 
	- Southern Poverty -> "Book Help" was *waste of time*
	- Focus: **Industrial Education** to make a living (*vocational*)
	- Believed women should following traditional roles
	- gained patronage via private support/influence
- ### View on White Society?
	- *Avoided confrontation*
	- education, *hard work*, and **respectability** -> erase prejudice
## W.E.B. DuBois
- Born in Massachusetts
- College Undergrad @ Fisk University (Memphis), Harvard
- Graduate Studies in Germany
- Professor + Author
- ### Strategies + Goals to improve Black lives?
	- Pro-action, fought for equal treatment and opportunity
	- "Compromise failed policy"
- ### View on White Society?
	- Saw reality of compromise as => Segregation & lynching
	- Strong racial stance
		- Led NAACP
		- Editor of 'Crisis' magazine
