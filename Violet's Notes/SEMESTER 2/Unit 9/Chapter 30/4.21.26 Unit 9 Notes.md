# 1999 WTO Protest - "Battle of Seattle"
- ***WTO*** -> *World Trade Organization*
	- Able to make decisions regarding *trade* across the world
- ***Issues***
	- WTO decisions...
		- negatively affecting environment
		- allowed exploitation
	- WTO seen as favoring corporate interest over people & the environment
- **"Battle of Seattle"
	- 25k protesters, block hotels, streets, preventing WTO meetings from going on
		- majority nonviolent
			- few looting used as justification for police violence?
	- protesters were *environmentalists*, *workers' rights* activists
- **City Action**
	- Nonviolent protesters attacked by police w/ pepper spray
		- -> Police run out of spray
		- Police also used rubber bullets, flashbangs
			- -> Many hospitalized
	- Protesters, bystanders, even journalists arrested
	- Curfew placed
	- Mayor *banned gas masks*
- **Result**
	- Head of WTO *suspends meetings*
# 9/11 2001 Terrorist Attacks
- 8:45 AM -> Boeing 767 crashes into north tower of the **World Trade Center**, NYC
	- Created giant burning hole at ~80th floor of skyscraper (110 floors total)
	- 100s instantly killed & 100s trapped in higher floors
- Initially believed to be a freak accident, <u>until 2nd & 3rd plane hit south tower & pentagon</U>
	- 2nd plane caused explosion, hitting ~60th floor, causing more death
- => Twin Towers collapse, steel beams melted from jet fuel
- 9:45 AM -> 3rd American Airlines plane crashes into west side of the **Pentagon military headquarters**
	- all 64 people on plane killed, alongside 125 at the pentagon (military & civilian)
- 10:10 AM -> 4th plane, Flight 93 hijacked as well, but crashed in western Pennsylvania
	- Likely crashed due to passengers fighting hijackers
	- All 44 people aboard killed
	- Theorized to have been targeting the White House, Camp David presidential retreat, or a nuclear power plant
## Attackers
- Attacks done by terrorist organization **al-Qaeda**, led by Saudi fugitive **Osama bin Laden**
	- Theorized reasoning for attack was U.S.'s support of Israel, involvement in Persian Gulf War, and continued presence in the Middle East
- Some of the 19 terrorists were living in U.S. for more than a year, hijackers having taken flight lessons in U.S. schools

### Afghanistan War (2001-2021)
- Started immediately after 9/11 attacks
#### Goals
- Destroying **Taliban** (extreme right wing faction ruling AF, and harboring al-Qaeda)
- Rebuild AF institutions
- Reduce/end Taliban & insurgent attacks, reduce civilian deaths
#### Timeline
- 2001 -> Taliban rule ended
- 2002-2008 -> Taliban militarily defeated, institutions rebuilt
- 2008-2009 -> Increase in U.S. troops in AF
- 2011 -> More responsibilities given to AF military
	- Attempts to reduce attacks & civilian deaths failed
### Iraq War/ Second Persian Gulf War (2003-2011)
- **Reasons for War**
	- Believed that Iraq harbored terrorists & supported al-Qaeda
	- President **George W. Bush** believed that Iraq was holding and creating *weapons of mass destruction*
		- Claims were later proven wrong
#### 2003 
- November 8th, 2002 -> *Resolution 1441* forces Iraq to readmit inspectors, and to comply w/ all previous resolutions
- early 2003 -> Bush & Br. Prime Minister Blair declare Iraq as hindering UN inspections and still holding weapons
	- French President & German Chancellor attempt to give Iraq more time to comply, citing Iraq's compliance
- March 17, 2003 -> Bush declares end to diplomacy, ultimatum issued to Iraqi President Saddam, giving him *48 hours* to leave Iraq
	- Bush's decision opposed by many countries
