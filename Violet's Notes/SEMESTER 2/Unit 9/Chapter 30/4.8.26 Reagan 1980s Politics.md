# 1980 Election
## Republicans
- Against increasingly liberal social agenda of Democrats
- Fueled by rise of militant & well-organized *Evangelical Christianity*
	- => Most Southern states begin voting majority Republican
- **"New Right"** => Conservative Christians, Southern whites, affluent ethnic suburbanites, & young conservatives, *support Ronald Reagan*
- **Reagan's "Law and Order" Platform**
	- Stricter laws against crime, drugs, porn
	- Oppose easy-access abortion
	- Increase defense spending
	- Cut tax rates, especially for the wealthy
- <U>Reagan did not reduce the size or scope of the Fed. Govt. and its power</u>
## Democrats
- Support environmental legislation, limiting economic development
- Aim to halt production of *nuclear weapons and power plants*
- Pro-choice movement emerges in 80s
- Affirmative Action => Racial quotas, aim to add diversity to workforce & "balance" it
# Reagan
## Economic Plans
- **Trickle Down**/**Supply Side**/*free-market* economics
	- Cut taxes
		- Cut taxes on the rich = invest money and create jobs (not true)
	- Cut social welfare spending
		- "Welfare queen" stereotype
	- Deregulate businesses
	- Defense spending
### Outcome
- Deficits (as % of GDP) dropped significantly during Reagan presidency
- increased National **Debt**
- increased wealth gap(?) -> & <u>didn't "trickle down"</U>
## Foreign Policy
- **Strategic Defense Initiative** (**SDI**)
	- Promotes expensive, *unproven* plan (Star Wars) using lasers & satellites to protect U.S. from missile attacks
	- *Outspend* the "Evil Empire", <u>refuse to reduce defense programs</u>
- **Gorbachev (USSR) Reforms**
	- Glasnost (Openness), Perestroika (Restructuring) => help save country
		- => Collapse of USSR, becomes just Russia
## Scandal
- **1987 Iran-Contra Affair** 
	- U.S. traded arms to free hostages from Iran
		- Portion of $ was used to support Contras in Nicaragua fighting (Communist) Sandinistas
			- Congress wouldn't allow previous drug $ funding by CIA
	- Reagan claimed he would never negotiate w/ terrorists => Negotiated w/ terrorists
