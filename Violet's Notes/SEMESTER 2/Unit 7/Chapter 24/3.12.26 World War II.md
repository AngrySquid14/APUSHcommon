# Societal Groups
## Women
- 12 mil. men serve in military => women needed to fill industry roles
	- 6 mil. women worked in war
- Seattle -> women make up 40% of Boeing workforce during war
- Volunteer w/ Red Cross
- Serve military -> nurses, clerks, technicians, etc.
- "Rosie the Riveter"
## Jewish Am.
- 550k men & women serve military
- sought relief of immigration quotas (Eastern Europe) after learning about Jewish extermination
- Bill ended by Congress (1939), would've allowed 20k Jewish children into America
- Motivated to serve in war to fight against Nazism
	- Led to decline in hostility from Christian Am.
## JP Am.
- After *Pearl Harbor*, JP Am. face discrimination, blamed for nations actions.
- ### Japanese Internment
	- **Executive Order 9066**, issued by FDR Feb. 19th, 1942
		- authorized *forced removal and internment* of JP Am. *on the west coast ONLY*
	- result of paranoia following Pearl Harbor
	- ~120k JP Am. sent to poorly constructed camps in remote locations
	- 1944 -> Supreme Court (Korematsu v. United States) upheld constitutionality of Japanese internment
	- 1988 -> U.S. apologizes, gives $20k to 82k JP Am. or heirs
- ### JP Am. Soldiers in WWII
	- ~18k *Nisei* (2nd Gen) fought in segregated Japanese unit
	- Units were highly acclaimed, received many awards
		- 9,500 Purple Hearts, 7 Presidential Distinguished Unit Citations
## Native Am.
- 12k serve in WWI, 44k serve in WWII
- 150k participate in War Effort
	- *Highest record of service per capita* of all ethnic groups in America
- Natives considered themselves proud of their country, hence fighting in "the white man's war"
	- Native traditions & culture of honor also tie into desire to aid nation
- ### Navajo Code Talkers
	- U.S. Military struggling b/c Japanese kept breaking their codes
	- U.S. Navajo Marines used their native language to transmit messages
		- Code was never broken by the Japanese
		- Code talkers helped U.S. win war in Pacific
## African Am.
- **Double V Campaign** -> Victory over fascism in Europe + Victory over racism at home
- Faced discrimination & segregation in WWII
	- Only POC made to load ammunition
	- all 900k African Am. served in segregated units
	- Whites refused blood if it was from a black person
- **A. Philip Randolph** -> Pushed Roosevelt for racial equality, threatened *a march on DC, leading to executive action*
- Executive order 8802 (1941) -> *Prohibited racial discrimination in the defense industry*
- ### Tuskegee Airmen
	- First black U.S. military pilots, all-black unit
	- War Department set up system w/ high expectations, expected spots to be hard to fill
		- However, received many applications from qualified men
	- End of War -> Tuskegee Airmen credited w/ *109 aircrafts shot down, one patrol boat, and multiple fuel dumps, trucks, and trains destroyed*
	- Were sometime known as "Redtails" because of crimson paint on vertical stabilizers of planes
		- were often requested by Allied Bombers, not knowing they were all black
## Latinos
- 500k+ Latinos serve (mainly Mexican)
	- Were not segregated like African Am. & Japanese Am.
- Earned many medals, distinguished
- 1000s (men & women) serve on home front -> mines, railroads, shipyard, agriculture, factories, etc.
	- Shortage of manual labor -> *Bracero Program* allows 125k Mexican workers to come to U.S. for *Agriculture & Railroads*
- Latino labor *crucial to U.S. Wartime effort*
- ### 1943 Zoot Suit Riots
	- Latinos youths rebel against Anglo culture, wore zoot suits
	- Garments rationed during WWII, making zoot suit manufacturing prohibited
		- Wearing suits drew attention, often negative?
	- 11 U.S. Navy Sailors claim to be attacked by Mexican kids in *Los Angeles* -> Sailors & Marines invade East LA, target those wearing zoot suits
		- Lasted 2 nights, Mexicans beaten and arrested
## LGBTQ
- Wartime migration -> new opportunities to establish communities
- LGBTQ still mostly remain hidden due to religious & social discrimination
- Some gay neighborhoods form in NYC, San Francisco, LA, etc.
	- More open atmosphere during war
- Military tries to screen recruits for sexuality, doesn't work, gay communities form in military.
