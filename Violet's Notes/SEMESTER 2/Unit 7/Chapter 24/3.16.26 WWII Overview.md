# WWII Overview
## Mass Mobilization
- Provided:
	- Large # of troops for the *war effort*
	- Increased workforce, ending the Depression
	- *Opportunities* for women + minorities to improve socioeconomic positions
		- Women -> work in factories & health care
	- African Am. -> work in factories & serve in military
## Wartime Experiences
- ### Japanese Internment
	- Forced movement of Japanese Am. (2/3rds Nisei) to camps during war
	- Upheld by the Supreme Court, *Korematsu v. U.S.*
- ### Challenges to Civil Liberties
	- *Zoot Suit Riots* -> Conflict between Mexican Am. and U.S. military in SoCal
- ### Debates over Race & Segregation
	- A. Philip Randolph (civil rights leader) -> helped lead to *Executive Order 8802* -> ended discrimination in defense industries
	- Double V Campaign -> Victory over Fascism abroad, Victory over racism at home
	- Military remained *segregated until the Korean War* (E.O. 9981)
- ### Decision to Drop Atomic Bomb
	- Many arguments
		- -> to save Am. lives (100k loss w. Japan invasion)
		- -> b/c invested $2 billion to build it, should use it
		- -> to demonstrate power to Soviet Union
		- -> to end the war quickly
## Why Allies Won
- ### Political & Military Cooperation
	- U.S., G.B. France, and Soviet Union
	- Tehran Conference -> "Big Three" meeting; agree to invasion of Europe in 1944
- ### Industrial Production
	- U.S. factories were dedicated to war effort
	- Ford & GM -> convert to military production
- ### Tech & Science Advances
	- *Manhattan Project* -> $2 billion project to develop the *atomic bomb*
	- *Sonar* -> used against German submarines
- ### Popular Commitment to Advancing Democracy
	- *Atlantic Charter* -> Churchill & Roosevelt meet privately, set goals for post-WWII world (Before Pearl Harbor)
		- Ideas: self-determination, economic cooperation
## U.S. Became Superpower Because...
- ### Dominant Role in Allied Victory & Peace Settlements
	- Creation of the *United Nations* in NY
	- U.S. became a permanent member of the *UN Security Council*
	- <u>Departure from Washington's Farewell Address</U> (involvement in foreign affairs)
- ### Europe & Asia Severely Damaged by War
	- *No destruction on U.S. soil*
# WWII Homefront
## Rationing for the War Effort
- Allocate scare goods -> meat, butter, sugar, coffee, shoes
- Stamp & point systems
- Complex *gasoline rationing*
- Emergence of a Black market
### War Rationing Coupon Books
- Shortages => U.S. OPA establishes rationing system
- Americans given ration books, contained removable stamps good for certain items
	- Red Stamps => meat, butter, fats, cheese
	- Blue Stamps => fruits, vegetables, soup, baby good
### Shortage Reasons
- Processed & canned food was reserved for shipping overseas to military + allies
- fresh food transportation limited b/c of gasoline & tire rationing (priority was transporting soldiers)
- import restrictions -> less coffee & sugar
## Office of Price Administration
- Goal: *limit wartime inflation*
- Established "ceiling prices" for many goods
- Rationed scare goods & consumer staples
- Rationing *ended with war*, dissolved in *1947*
# Pearl Harbor, Hawaii (1941)
- Pearl Harbor, Hawaii -> U.S. Military Base
- Japan attacks Harbor w/ bomber aircraft, drops bombs on battleships, sinking & damaging 19
	- USS Arizona sank
- Attacked in 2 waves -> 167 planes in second attack
- Large casualties, 2403 killed
- U.S. fighter planes were parked wing-to-wing, with no fuel, couldn't take off
- *9 other sites* attacked in Pacific & Asia on December 7th & 8th
- ### Why Attack U.S.?
	- Japan took Manchuria, Korea, parts of Indochina to gain power
	- Summer 1940 -> U.S. responds w/ Japan trade embargo of oil, scrap iron, fuel, etc.
	- 9/27/40 -> Japan signs *Tri Partite Pact* w/ Germany & Italy against USSR
- ### Warnings Beforehand
	- Japanese Ambassador to U.S. sent message of declared war w/ U.S. an hour before, but was delayed by translations, etc.
	- Govt. intercepted messages of Japan discussing attack, *but chose not to share with Navy*
		- thought distance was too far for Japan to fly
