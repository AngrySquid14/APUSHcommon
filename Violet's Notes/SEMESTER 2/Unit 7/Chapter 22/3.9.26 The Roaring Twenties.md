# The Roaring 20's
- ## Main Traits
	- Cultural & Economic prosperity
		- Birth of modern America -> great change in culture
		- More people living in cities than rural areas
	- Republican Power
	- Conflict
- ## 1920s Trends
	- Economic expansion
		- Stock market boom
	- Mass Productions
		- Assembly line
	- *Creation of Automobiles* (Ford)
	- *African American flight* (South -> North)
	- Ailing Agriculture
- ## Why the 20s Prospered
	- Increase in factories & businesses -> more jobs
	- Wages grow by ~1/3 1900-1929
	- **Investment** -> Stocks very profitable
	- Consumer Economy and Technology
		- Electricity -> radios, washing machines, vacuum cleaners, refrigerators
			- 70% of homes have electricity by end of 20s
- ## Automobiles
	- *Assembly lines* => mass produced cars => *affordable* cars
		- => roads & highways built, people move to suburbs
	- Motels, gas stations, restaurants built
	- Grew glass, steel, & rubber industries
- ## Agriculture
	- Agricultural depression (early 20s) => *urban migration*
		- U.S. farmers lose markets in Europe after the war ends
	- Agricultural efficiency = better tech = fewer workers needed => *urban migration*
	- Farming *no longer prosperous*
	- Black Am. continue to endure poverty
		- still trapped in Sharecropping system
		- *1915* => boll weevil wipes out cotton
			- => white landowners go broke, force African am. off land
- ## Why did Black People Leave the South?
	- Lack of jobs
	- Discrimination & terror
		- Segregation/Jim Crow, lynching, KKK
	- Movement north = **The Great Migration**
- ## Migration Outcomes
	- Black ghettoes form (i.e. Harlem, NY) w/ *distinct black culture*
	- Northern whites resent black migrants => Race Riots 1919
		- resentment because of labor competition and dislike of black am. moving into white neighborhoods
- ## Black Nationalism
	- **Marcus Garvey** (Jamaican immigrant)
		- establishes the *Universal Negro Improvement Association*
		- Black pride
		- advocation *for* racial segregation because of Black superiority
		- believed Black Am. should return to Africa
			- Purchased ship to start *Black Star* line
		- Eventually charged w/ fraud, found guilty, deported to Jamaica
- ## Culture
	- Radio
		- KDKA Pittsburgh GE, Westinghouse, RCA form NBC
	- Celebrities
		- Babe Ruth, Ty Cobb, Charles Lindbergh, Jack Dempsey
	- *Jazz*
		- Flappers -> Short skirts, heavier makeup, smoking, partying
		- Artists -> Louis Armstrong, Duke Ellington
	- *Harlem Renaissance*
		- Art & literary movement, African Am. pride increases
		- Spreads beyond black culture
- ## Women
	- Post WWI, want to get out and enjoy independence
	- Dishwashers & washing machines invented => easier housework
	- Many women waited for men who died in war -> wait no longer, go out and party
	- #### Flappers
		- Had their own slang
		- young women, challenge social norms, adopt new fashions
			- Showing leg, no corsets, high heels
			- Short hair -> Bob, Shingles (slicked down hair)
		- Drank and smoked (often previously reserved for men)
		- Wore more makeup
		- More promiscuous (casual sex?)
	- ## Challenging Social Norms
		- Young women seek equal status w/ men, be assertive
		- Women see marriage as *equal partnership*
			- Housework & child-rearing *still a woman's job*
		- Women subjected to double standard of having less sexual freedom
			- Elders disapprove of young women's behavior
		- Formal courtship -> *Casual dating*
	- ### Women Shed Old Roles
		- 1920 -> **19th Amendment** => Women have federal vote
		- **New Work Opportunities**
			- Post-war, women workers replaced with men
			- Female college grads -> teachers, nurses, librarians
			- Demand for female salesclerks & factory workers grows
			- *few women become managers, paid less than men*
	- ### The Changing Family
		- Birthrate drops b/c more birth-control information
		- Manufactured products & public services give housewives more freedom to focus on their pastimes and families *instead of housework*
		- Marriages even more based on *romantic love & companionship*
		- Working-class educated women juggle both family and work
		- More rebellious children
- # Conflict
	- ### Anti-Immigrant
		- N. European immigrants: fear of new religions in U.S. -> Judaism, Orthodox beliefs, Catholicism
		- Nativism
		- #### Sacco-Vanzetti Trial
			- Italian immigrants, convicted and executed for murder
				- trial was unfair, unknown if they were guilty or innocent
	- Fear of immigrants (SE Europe) => Red Scare
	- ### Palmer Raids
		- Form antiradical division (FBI) and raid radical spaces
	- ### Rise of the KKK
		- significant increase in power
		- **Beliefs**
			- Racist
			- Antisemitic
			- Anti-immigrant
			- Anti-Catholic
			- Anti-women's suffrage
			- Anti-bootleggers
			- Pro-W.A.S.P.
				- White Anglo-Saxon Protestant
		- The Birth of A Nation
	- ### Prohibition
		- 18th Amendment & Volstead Act
			- Temperance movement finally caused social change
			- Drunkenness seen as lowering productivity, inefficient
		- **Was not successful**
			- **Difficult to enforce**
				- Homemade alcohol
				- Alcohol smuggling made billions $
				-  led to rise of gangsters (Al Capone) & organized crime
				- Doctors handed out "medicinal" alcohol
				- Police & judges bribed
