# The Stock Market Boom
- Electricity, Car, Entertainment industry growth => 1920's business growth
- People make $ buying stock in growing companies (like .com bubble?)
	- Stock prices rise, more people put money into stocks
		- 1928 -> Stock prices rise quick enough to borrow & repay money very quickly
# The Stock Market Crash
- **1929**
	- *September 3rd* -> Stock prices start falling
	- *October 23rd* -> Stock price index (Dow Jones) drops ~$21/hr
		- Boom was over
	- *October 24th* -> "**Black Thursday**" -> Stock prices drop drastically, *people lose $3 billion in a single day*
	- *October 29th* -> "**Black Tuesday**" -> 16+ mil. shares sold, *market crashes*
## Ranching Issues
- Crop & livestock prices dropping even before crash
- Reduced earnings => Farmers in debt, lose farms
- **Mid-1930s Dust Bowl**
	- Drought, removal of natural gasses, overgrazing => ruined Great Plains farmland
	- Massive wind storms follow, daylight -> darkness
		- Winds & Drought made lands *unfarmable*
## Other Issues
- Low factory worker wages
- *1929-1932* -> 5000 Banks fail
	- Banks invested most $ in stock market
	- People + Businesses with money in banks lost it
## Unemployment
- *1932* -> 12 mil. Americans unemployed (25% of workforce)
- 1-2 mil. homeless
- Massive unemployment in cities (NYC, Chicago, Cleveland)
## Families
- Men ashamed of not being able to support family => abandon home
- ~250k children leave home, travel country for better life
	- "Wild Boys" & "Hoover Tourists" => boys riding on freight cars to travel country
# F.D.R and The New Deal
- ### Context
	- Depression continues, increasing unemployment and collapsing bank system
	- Americans want new leadership -> elect Franklin Delano Roosevelt over Herbert Hoover (*1932*)
- ### The New Deal
	- F.D.R takes office (*1933*), promises a "**New Deal**" for Americans suffering through the Depression
	- Democrat majorities in House & Senate => 15 major bills passed in first 100 days => *New Deal* created
		- New Deal => created 100s of organizations to provide *Relief, Reform, & Recovery*
			- Relief -> direct help for unemployed & poor
			- Reform -> Change system so crisis couldn't happen again
			- Recovery -> bring economy back to normal levels
