# Ratification of the Treaty of Versailles
- ## President Wilson
	- Want to establish U.S. membership in the *League of Nations*
- ## Irreconcilables
	- Wouldn't accept *any portion* of the treaty
- ## Reservationists
	- Led by Republican Senator Henry Lodge
	- Would agree to peace treaty *if it could be amended to include "reservations"*
		- League of Nations article X -> Having to send U.S. troops if member is attacked (*Collective Security*)
# Wilson's 14 Points
- **Rejected points**
	- #2 -> Freedom of the Seas
	- #3 -> Free Trade
	- #4 -> Reduce Stockpiles of Weapons
	- #5 -> Provide liberty to their colonies
	- etc. (some points were adjusted)
- **Accepted points**
	- #14 -> Association of Nations provides security and ensures peace
**U.S. ended up rejecting the treaty, and *therefore their participation in the League of Nations***
