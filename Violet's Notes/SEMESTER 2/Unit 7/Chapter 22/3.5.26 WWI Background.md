# Balkans
- **Berlin Congress**(*1878*) => Austria-Hungary has right to *administer* Bosnia + Herzegovina
	- later (*1908*) incorporates them into A-H empire
	- -> Serbia & Russia (*Slavic* nations) not happy
- *1912-1913* 2 Balkan Wars, involve Serbia, Bulgaria, Greece, Turkey
# Start of WWI
- *June 28th, 1914* => **Archduke Franz Ferdinand** (A-H) assassinated by *Serbian Nationalist*
	- German emperor vows to stand w/ A-H to seek justice from Serbia
		- -> A-H takes this as a *blank check* to back all retaliation against Serbia
## Austria-Hungary Response
- A-H gives ultimatum to Serbia => dismiss anti-A-H officials, allow A-H to investigate murder in Serbia, respond in 48hrs
	- Serbia accepts all *except allowing A-H officials into Serbia* (violation of independence)
		- -> A-H mobilizes army -> Russia *partially* mobilizes army to aid Serbia (had alliance/deal to protect Serbia)
## It Gets Worse
- Britain's "power conference" to resolve issue rejected
- *July 28th, 1914* Austria-Hungary *declares war* on Serbia
	- =>*July 29th, 1914* Russia fully mobilizes army
		- Germany asks to halt moves, receives no response, *August 1st, 1914* Germany *declares war* on Russia
- *August 3rd, 1914* Germany *declares war* on France (*part of **Schlieffen Plan***)
	- *August 3rd* Germans cross through Belgium (neutral) to France
- *August 4th, 1914* Britain *declares war* on Germany (made deal in 1830 *to protect Belgian neutrality*)
- ### TL;DR Timeline
	- #### 1914
		- July 28th -> A-H ✕ Serbia
		- July 29th -> Russia half-mobilizes
		- August 1st -> Germany ✕ Russia
		- August 3rd -> Germany ✕ France
			- Germany crosses thru Belgium
		- August 4th -> Britain ✕ Germany
	- #### 1917
		- April 2nd -> U.S. ✕ Germany
## How Did U.S. Move from Neutrality to War?
- TL;DR beef w/ Germany over not sinking ships => President Wilson declares war on Germany
	- Germany pledges to not sink ships (Wilson hoped for peace, and Germany had been sinking passenger ships), but then sinks the Sussex (French passenger ship)
