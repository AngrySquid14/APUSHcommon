# Spanish-American War
- ## Causes
	- **2 NY Newspapers in competition** => NY Journal vs. NY World
	- Cuban Freedom rebels burn plantations, blow up trains
		- Spain (200k army) responds w/ brutality & *concentration camps*
		- In response, NY Newspapers inflame American public, using tabloid headlines ("**Yellow Journalism**") abt Spanish atrocities
			- Increases newspaper sales greatly, doubles NY Journal subscribers
	- -> USS Maine sent *to protect American interests in Cuba*
		- explodes in Havana Harbor, Spain blamed, Americans outraged
	- President McKinley & Congress declare war (April 19th, 1898) w/ Spain
		- **Teller Amendment** => NO conquering Cuba
- ## Reasons for War?
	- Spanish brutality towards Cuban rebels & civilians, *Americans support Cuban independence*
	- "Yellow Press/Journalism" -> exaggerated media accounts *promoted war*
		- *de Lome letter* => private letter from Spanish minister mocking President
	- Spain blamed for USS Maine explosion (260 Am. killed)
	- Protect business investments ($50 mil.), *stabilize markets*
- ## Spanish American War Battles
	- U.S. Navy *blockades* Cuba, captures Guantanamo Bay
	- 17k soldiers head ashore, near Santiago
		- soldiers suffer b/c hot, humid climate
			- uniforms made of wool = suffering
		- also, weaponry out of date, food rotten & contaminated
	- 9th & 10th All-Black Cavalry units ("Buffalo Soldiers") + Roosevelt's "Rough Riders" storm San Juan/Kettle Hills, take high ground above Santiago
- ## Territorial Gains from the War
	- U.S. War victory => peace treaty made, Spanish forced to relinquish territories
		- Spain cedes *Guam, Puerto Rico, and the Philippines*
		- Spain loses claims to Cuba
		- *Note:* U.S. also annexes Hawaii during this time, unrelated to war
	- => U.S. becomes predominant in *Latin America*
		- U.S. pursues economic interests in Asia (Philippines, Guam)
- ## Battles in the Philippines
	- Commodore Dewey + Naval fleet => surprise attack Spanish @ Manila Bay, Spanish destroyed
	- Filipino Nationalists (Emilio Aguinaldo) => defeat Spanish Army to become independent, U.S. arrives, Spain surrenders
		- Aguinaldo *thought U.S. was aiding in independence, like w/ Cuba*
	- U.S. decides to keep the Philippines (1899), Filipinos use guerilla warfare to fight Americans (insurrection)
		- => U.S. burns entire villages, destroys fields, captures & murders rebels
			- Aguinaldo eventually captured (Spring 1901)
	- **Results**: 5k American deaths, 200k Filipino deaths
		- U.S. sent 100k troops, spent $400 mil.
- ## Overall Effects
	- Increased imperialism debate
	- Increased Patriotism
	- Expanded markets
