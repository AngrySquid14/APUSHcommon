# The Gilded Age
- Rapid industrialization + immigration => growth of cities
- Govt. corruption, *especially on the local level*
- Effects of Reconstruction failure evident
- *Some* previous attempts at reform
# **The Progressive Era**
- ## Who Were The Progressives?
	- **Urban Middle Class**
		- Women
		- Have free time + financial resources, so can push for reform
	- **"Muckrakers"**
		- Investigative journalists
		- Aim to inform public
	- **Politicians**
		- Federal, state, local levels
- ## Progressive Objectives
	- Attack *political corruption* + disenfranchisement
		- women's suffrage
		- secret ballot
	- Sponsor *urban social change*
		- *safer tenements* 
		- better working conditions
	- Address *social injustice*, moral reform
		- civil rights
		- temperance
	- Promote economic *equality*
		- "trust busting"
		- banking reform
	- Environmental preservation & conservation
		- National parks
		- resource conservation
- ## Why Progressive Movement Now?
	- Economy grew w/o considering impact on individuals, cities, environment
	- Growing middle class
	- Journalists become interested in reporting issues
	- Growing conflict between labor & management
	- Strong political leadership
		- Robert La Follette, Teddy Roosevelt, etc.
# **Evaluating the Success of the Progressive Era**
- ## Successes?
	- Political corruption -> *referendum*, secret ballot, 19th Amendment (1920, women's vote)
	- Urban social change -> State laws mandating safer working conditions, settlement houses, liberal divorce laws
	- Economic equality -> Clayton *Antitrust* Act, *Federal Reserve*
	- Environmental protection -> National Parks system, Forest Reserve Act
	- Social Injustice/Moral Reform -> 18th Amendment (Prohibition)
		- Civil rights, not so much
- ## Reform Goals
	- ### Efforts
		- NCL -> seek labor protection, better hours
		- Social Justice -> NAACP, Niagara Movement (legal action), Ida B. Wells' Anti-Lynching campaign
	- ### Results
		- Women/Labor protection => State level, widows pension, 10 hr. workday *for women* (Oregon v. Muller)
		- Democracy => State level, referendums, recall officials (La Follette -> Wisconsin)
		- Government Intervention => Economy -> set shipping rates (ICC/Hepburn Act)
	- **Racial Justice -> no results until Civil Rights Era (1950s-60s)**
While the Progressive Era succeeded in achieving its goals of increasing democracy, land preservation, and partially labor protection, the Progressive Era failed to see substantial progress in racial justice.
