# 1st Party System (*1792-1824*)
## Federalists
#### Leaders
- Alexander Hamilton, **John Adams**, **George Washington** (kind of unaffiliated)
#### Beliefs
- *Central Power*
- Loose interpretation of Constitution
- Pro-trade, Pro-British, Pro-Industry
- Wealthy elites -> *Do not trust voters*
#### Collapse
- *Opposition to War of 1812*
	- Hartford Convention -> 2/3rds to pass acts
- Replaced by Whig Party
## Democratic Republicans
#### Leaders
- **Thomas Jefferson**, **James Madison**, **James Monroe**, **John Quincy Adams**
#### Beliefs
- *State Power*
- Strict interpretation of Constitution
- **Agrarian**, Pro-French
- Trust in voters
	- Related to support for French Rev.
#### Collapse
- **Andrew Jackson** focuses more on Democratic aspect b/c more "common people" voting 
	- b/c <u>property ownership no longer required to vote</u>
# 2nd Party System (*1828-1854*)
## Whigs
#### Leaders
- **Henry Clay**, William Henry Harrison, John Tyler, Zachary Taylor, Millard Filmore
#### Origins
- Name -> Whigs: British party that *opposed tyranny* ("King Andrew")
#### Beliefs
- **Henry Clay's American System**
	- BUS, Internal Improvements, Tariffs
- 2nd G.A. Reforms
#### Collapse
- **Kansas-Nebraska Act** (1854)
	- Furthered North/South split over expansion of slavery in the West
- Replaced by anti-slavery Republican Party
## Democrats
#### Leaders
- **Andrew Jackson**, Martin Van Buren, *James Polk*, Franklin Pierce, James Buchanan
#### Origins
- Name shortened to Democrats b/c of increased voting franchise w/ non-property owners
#### Beliefs
- Oppose BUS, Tariffs, Infrastructure
- <u>Individual choice over reforms</u>
# 3rd Party System (*1854-1890s*)
## Republicans
#### Leaders
- **Abraham Lincoln**, *Ulysses S. Grant*
#### Beliefs
- Free Soil Party -> Oppose Slavery in new territories
- Abolitionists
- Northerners
## Democrats
#### Leaders
- *James Buchanan*
#### Beliefs
- Protect institute of Slavery
- Expand slavery to new territories
- Southerners
**Post-Reconstruction, little distinction between Republicans and Democrats**
# 4th Party System (*1896-1932*)
## Republicans
#### Leaders
- William McKinley, **Theodore Roosevelt**, William Howard Taft
#### Coalition
- Northern industry & **Laissez-Faire** => Big Business
#### 1920s
- Usher in 3 Republican Presidents w/ Conservative agendas
	- Response to Post-WWI Economic downturn, Red Scare, radicalism, strikes
	- *Warren G. Harding, Calvin Coolidge, Herbert Hoover*
## Democrats
#### Leaders
- **Woodrow Wilson**
#### Coalition
- Southerners
- Western Farmers
- Progressives
- Northern immigrants
# 5th Party System (*1932-1968*)
## Republicans
#### Leaders
- *Dwight D. Eisenhower*, **Richard M. Nixon**
#### Coalition
- Business
- Wealthy
#### Beliefs
- Tend to be moderate
- Limit govt. growth and regulations
## Democrats
#### Leaders
- **FDR**, *Harry Truman*, **John F. Kennedy**, **LBJ**
#### Coalition
- Unions
- Southerners
- Immigrants, Catholics, Jews
- Urbanites & intellectuals
#### Beliefs
- W/ the **"New Deal"**, FDR began Democratic policy of using fed. govt. to <u>aggressively confront America's social and economic issues (Civil rights, depression?)</u>
