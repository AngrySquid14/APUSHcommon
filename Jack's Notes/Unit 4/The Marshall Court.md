## Marbury vs. Madison
-  **Adams** appointed several judges right before Jefferson's inauguration:
	- **John Marshall to Chief Justice**
	- William Marbury to a lower fed. court
- Marbury's appointment **wasn't delivered until after admin. change**
	- -> Jefferson refused to appoint him
- #### Marbury appealed to the Supreme Court
	- Marbury thought that Marshall would give him judge
		- Both Fedsts.
	- Jefferson refused to give judge no matter what
	- **Marshall and the Supreme Court** said that the law that gave Marbury judgeship was unconstitutional
	- **GAVE SUPREME COURT JUDICIAL REVIEW**
		- -> the power to nullify laws that go against the constitution

## John Marshall
- He retained Fedst. policies into Jefferson's Presidency
- #### His Ideology:
	- **Three principles of Jurisprudence**
		- Judicial Authority
		- Supremacy of National Law
		- Traditional Property Rights
- #### Impact on Native Policy
	- **Marshall Court Decisions provided precedence for future tribal rights cases:**
		- Tribes have basic property rights
		- Tribes or sovereign entities
		- Federal Govt. has ultimate authority over tribal affairs.
		- **Tribal Authority (Federal Treaties)** overrule state laws
