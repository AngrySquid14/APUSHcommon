## Embargo Act of 1807:
- Prohibited ALL foreign commerce in order to compel other countries to respect American rights
	- During **Napoleonic Wars**
	- Neither British or French wanted the other to have American materials
- Jefferson thought that Europeans relied on American raw materials
	- However, Britain and France both found other raw materials
- **Overall** it was a failure
	- Made Jefferson look like a hypocrite
	- US navy patrolled American ports

## Non-Intercourse Act of 1809:
- Lifted embargoes on American shipping **EXEPT** for Britain and France
- Also mostly ineffective
- contributed to the upcoming War of 1812
