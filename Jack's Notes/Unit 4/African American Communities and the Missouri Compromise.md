## Missouri Compromise:
- **Background:**
	- 22 states in the country
		- 11 free - 11 slave
	- Missouri applied for statehood in 1819
	- Would upset balance of free/slave states
		- Change voting/govt dynamics
- **Tallmadge Amendment:**
	- Would allow gradual emancipation
	- House (North) favored, Senate (South) rejected
- **Missouri Compromise:**
	- Henry Clay proposed
	- **3 Parts:**
		- Missouri added as slave state
		- Maine taken from Mass. as a free state
		- All future states in LA territory above 36(degrees)30' would be free, below slave
- **Impacts:**
	- Balance remained at 12 free 12 slave
	- States were admitted in alternating slave free short term
	- Lasted 34 years -> Kansas Nebraska act

- As the US expanded beyond the LA Territory, slavery became the #1 issue.
- New compromises and court cases would attempt to address the issue
