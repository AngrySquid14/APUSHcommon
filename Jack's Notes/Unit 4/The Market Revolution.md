## Agricultural Innovation:
- **McCormick's Reaper** -> 1845
	- W/ a horse, 2 people could cut as much grain in a day as 7 men with sickles
	- Could plant 12 acres per day VS 2 acres by hand with less labor
- **John Deere** -> Illinois 1837
	- Steel plough is first device
		- Two blades cut bad Midwest soil
		- Made more land farmable
- **Cotton Gin** -> Eli Whitney, 1793
	- 1 man could remove seeds from 50 pounds of cotton per day
		- Prev. 1 pound/day
	- Hugely increased value of cotton
		- **Also during textile boom**
		- Demand for cotton was incredibly high
	- Led to increased use of enslaved labor
		- Expanding West to gain more land -> meet growing demand
	- **King Cotton**
