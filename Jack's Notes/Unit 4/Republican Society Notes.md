## Towards Republican Culture:
- Mercy Otis Warren advocated against patriarchy
- **Republican Marriages**:
	- Fewer arranged marriage -> not as much land conflict
	- Increase in marriage based on love, equality, respect
	- Husbands still had more power
	- **Decrease in family size**
		- Fewer single men
		- Later marriages
		- Birth control
		- Less Children -> Easier for parents
- **Republican Motherhood**:
	- Idea that mothers raise their children to be good citizens
	- Mothers took a more active role in education (primarily wealthy white women)

## Raising Republican Children:
- **Encouraging independence** vs **Authoritarianism**
	- Independence:
		- Children respected more
		- Primarily upper class, North East
	- Authoritarianism:
		- Ties into religious views
		- Generally rural, South, West
- **Debates over Education**:
	- Jefferson advocated education for Americans -> believed in Educated Voters
	- **More schools in North**
		- Denser Population
		- Southern Plantation owners focused on hiring tutors, not building schools
		- **Lead to higher literacy rates in the North**

## The Revolution & Slavery:
- Wartime loyalty allowed slaves to be freed in Maryland and Virginia
- **Manumission** and **Gradual Emancipation**:
	- Manumission -> freeing of slaves by owners
		- Virginia Manumission Act (1782) -> owners freed 10,000 people freed
- **Quakers and Enlightenment challenged slavery** -> free Blacks still faced lots of discrimination
- #### The South Defends Slavery:
	- **Slaveowners worried that potential for freedom would encourage rebellion among slaves**
	- Southern States tightened many restrictions:
		- Virginia amended manumission law in 1792, requiring special approval
	- 1800 -> Gabriel planned slave uprising in Virginia -> He and 30 others hanged
	- Eventually, Southern states **transitioned from necessary evil to positive** on slavery
	- Southern Dominance in Fed. Govt. protected slavery
- #### African Americans Speak Out:
	- **Cotton gin -> increased slave labor**
	- American Colonization Society -> proposed free slaves be sent to Africa (Liberia) 
		- Many African Americans disliked this idea
	- New black churches developed -> African Methodist Episcopal Church
