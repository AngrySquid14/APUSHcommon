#### **A Tumultuous Year**
- Civil Rights movement, Vietnam war, anti-war protests all in full swing
	- MLK and SCLC start Poor People's Campaign
	- Tet Offensive
	- RFK on track to win Democratic nomination in 1968
	- Chaos at Democratic Convention in Chicago

### Tet Offensive:
- January 1968 -> During Tet, N' Vietnamese and Viet Cong launch coordinated attacks on S' Vietnam
- **News Coverage shocked american public**
	- At this point there was nightly news coverage on Vietnam
	- Walter Cronkite coverage was hugely influential
 - **Many Americans began to see this as an unwinnable war**
