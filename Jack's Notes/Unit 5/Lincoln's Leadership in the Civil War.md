# Habeas Corpus:
- **The right of an arrested person to be brought before a judge to prevent false imprisonment**
	- Protected by the Constitution
- During the civil war, D.C. was surrounded by slave states
	- Many people in Maryland agreed with Confederacy
	- **Lincoln suspended habeas corpus in Maryland in 1861 to stop riots and protect the capital**
- As opposition to the war grew -> Lincoln expanded the suspension
	- General Orders No. 141 placed protestors under martial law
	- This suspended Habeas Corpus for the entire country

## Controversy around the suspension
- **Suspending Habeas Corpus is one of Lincoln's most controversial actions**
- 1863 -> Union Army arrested Peace Democrat Clement Vallandigham, shut down a pro-Democratic newspaper
	- Backlash followed
#### -  Election of 1864 and Habeas Corpus
- Lincoln v. McClellan
- Lincoln won -> justified suspension of Habeas Corpus as a wartime necessity

# The Emancipation Proclamation
- **Lincoln passes it mid war, using his wartime power**
	- Wasn't originally going to, but Confederacy wasn't backing down as expected
- Freed slaves in rebellion states
	- **DID NOT** free slaves in the loyal slave states
- Made the civil war a war about slavery
- Weakened the Confederacy
	- Freed enslaved people were able to escape, join Union Army
	- Discouraged European allies from supporting Confederacy
- **Eventually led to the 13th Amendment -> banning slavery**
