## U.S. v. Cruikshank (1876)
- Issue:
	- **Stemmed from the Colfax Massacre**
	- Case addressed whether fed. charges could be brought against white supremacists
	- Can the Fed. Govt apply private law to individual citizens
- Decision:
	- Ruled that the 14th Amendment only restricted state action, not the actions of individuals
	- Limited Federal enforcement of civil rights protections
- Impact:
	- Undermined federal ability to protect African Americans from racial violence
	- Allowed private acts of racial terrorisms to go unpunished

## Civil Rights Cases (1883)
- Issue:
	- Constitutionality of Civil Rights Act of 1875 -> banned racial discrimination in public accommodations
- Decision:
	- the Act was deemed unconstitutional, private businesses could discriminate
	- only govt. businesses couldn't discriminate
- Impact:
	- Legalized racial discrimination in many public spaces
	- Paved way for Jim Crow laws

## 15th Amendment and Women
- Suffrage movement split into two factions
	- Over supporting 15th Amendment despite exclusion of women
- **American Women Suffrage Association (AWSA)**
	- Supported 15th Amendment
	- Viewed it as critical step forward for civil rights
	- Believed women could "ride the wave" of progress and their suffrage would be achieved soon after black men
	- Strategy was more gradual -> worked within system with male allies to change policy over time
- **National Women Suffrage Association (NWSA)**
	- Opposed 15th Amendment
	- Argued that universal suffrage should include both women and African American men
	- Some thought white women more important than Black men
	- Strategy was bolder -> aimed for new amendment/rewriting of 15th amendment to enable female suffrage immediately
