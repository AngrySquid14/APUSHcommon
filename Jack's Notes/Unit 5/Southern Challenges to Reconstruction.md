### Black Codes:
- **The US govt passed acts intended to give newly freed African Americans rights and secure their ability to be free.**
	- The South consistently tried to deny these rights, and passed laws to avoid following them
		- **African Americans constantly found ways to help fight back against these laws** (NAACP, HBCU system, volunteer firefighters to respond to racially motivated violence)
- **13th Amendment (End of slavery)** -> Sharecropper system
- **14th Amendment (Citizenship)** -> Black codes, Jim Crow Laws
- **15th Amendment (Right to vote)** -> Poll tax, literacy test, Grandfather clause
- Black Codes attempted to recreate slavery:
	- Tried to keep African Americans economically and socially dependent
	- **Key Laws:**
		- Apprenticeship laws:
			- Black children could be forced to work for former enslavers
		- African Americans could be punished for:
			- Not having a job
			- Spending money "irresponsibly"
			- Very minor offenses

### Jim Crow Laws:
- Name comes from a song and dance character from blackface minstrel shows
- **Laws separated the races in the south:**
	- Mandated separate public facilities/infrastructure
	- Denied citizenship rights and equality provided by 14th Amendment
- **Black codes were enacted right after Civil War** -> Jim Crow laws put in place after they were repealed, and lasted much longer
