#### The South:
- **States' Rights:**
	- Many southerners didn't want strong central govt.
	- Felt more loyal to their state than CSA (Confederate States)
- **Conscription:**
	- The Confederacy implemented the first draft law -> 1862
	- **Any person who owned more than 20 slaves could get out of service**
		- Very few southerners owned slaves
		- Very unpopular with poor whites
		- **"Rich man's war but a poor man's fight"** <- important

#### The North:
- **Draft Riots:**
	- Conscription Act of 1863 -> allowed wealthy individuals to pay $300 for a substitute for the draft
	- Riots initially targeted govt. buildings
	- **Violence became a racially charged uprising**
		- African Americans, abolitionist symbols attacked
	- over 100 people died, **riots were stopped with fed. troops**
- **Riots in D.C.**
	- Led by Peace Democrats
	- **Criticized Lincoln's suspension of habeas corpus, other wartime measures**
	- Wanted peace immediately
