### **US Foreign Policy Goals:**
- Expand US power into other countries
	- **Gain access to trade with other countries**
- Spread US ideals/culture 
	- Democracy
	- Christianity
- "Frontier is closed"

### **Environmental impacts of Westward Expansion:**
- Mining:
	- Soil Erosion
	- Displacement of Wildlife
		- **As a result of deforestation**
	- Water Contamination
- Cattle Grazing:
	- Destruction of natural grasses
	- **lead to the Dust Bowl**
- Buffalo:
	- Destroyed Bison population
	- **Hugely hurt Native populations**

### **Homestead Act:**
- Gave citizens plots of land in the west as long as:
	- They "Improve it"
		- Build a house
		- Try to farm the land
- Land was very hard to farm
	- Awful weather
	- Pests
- Homesteaders didn't make much money, and equipment was expensive
- Homesteaders were also very isolated
