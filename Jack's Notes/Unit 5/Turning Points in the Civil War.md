1863 -> Turning Point for the North 
## The Battle of Gettysburg
- In Gettysburg PA
- Confederate goal -> make northerners feel the heat of the war a bit more
	- Thought that if north felt war they'd be more likely to get out of it
- Union saw the importance of this -> sent 90,000 soldiers to face 77,500 Confederates
- **Significance/Results:**
	- Lasted 3 days
	- Considered bloodiest battle ever fought on US soil
	- more than 50,000 Southern and Northern men dead or wounded
	- Turning point of Civil War -> Last battle fought on Union territory
### The Gettysburg Address:
- Emphasized:
	- That the sacrifice of the soldiers who died during the battle was important and impactful
	- That the Civil War is about freedom -> truly American principle
	- That the War isn't over -> The North will win

## The Battle of Vicksburg
- During the same time as Gettysburg
- **Grant's Strategy:**
	- Took control of Jackson -> Miss. state capital
	- Controlled the rail line into Vicksburg
		- Was able to siege the city 
-  **Significance:**
	- Grant is able to prove himself to Lincoln
		- -> Grant is put in command of the entire Union Forces
		- They follow a strategy of **Total War** going forward
			- Whole country is mobilized to war
			- Everything is a target for Union Army
				- Civilians, Factories, Infrastructure
			- Burning cities, Destroying rail lines
	- Union Army gained control of Miss. -> Anaconda Plan

## Sherman's March to the Sea
- September 2, 1864 -> William T. Sherman burned Atlanta to the ground
- **Goal:**
	- Take out Savannah, GA
		- Port city
	- Destroy everything in his path as he goes
	- Show southern civilians that "they could be hurt"
- Tore up railroad tracks
	- Harder to transport supplies
- Destroyed public buildings
- Vandalized homes
- Burned fields of crops
- **Southern Govt.:**
	- was very new
	- Hadn't really had a long time to establish themselves as a legitimate authority
	- **Sherman aimed to** -> convince the south that their govt. wasn't real/couldn't help them
- **Significance:**
	- March to the Sea + Lincoln getting reelected lead to the end of the war

## Why the North Won:
- **North:**
	- Greater Technology, Population, Resources
	- New Advantages:
		- Good military leaders -> Grant, Sherman
		- Steady leadership of Lincoln
		- Emancipation Proc -> no European support for Confederacy
- **South:**
	- Unsustainable
	- Used up Resources
	- Couldn't get European support
