### New Republic conflicts:
- Natives sought to limit American Expansion west
- **Battle of Fallen Timbers**:
	- lead to Treaty of Greenville, natives ceded much of the Ohio Valley (NW territory)
- British alliances with Natives lead to tensions with America -> pre War of 1812
- **Constitution gave no mention of Native policy**
- Treaties were often not enforced, ignored

### Relocation of Natives:
- Beginning in 1830s Natives were pushed further west
- **Indian Removal Act, Trail of Tears**
- Plains Indians:
	- Predominantly hunted buffalo
	- Land and Lifestyle was changed due to expansion

### *A Century of Dishonor (1881)*:
- Written by Helen Hunt Jackson
- Exposed Bad Indian policy/exploitation

## The Dawes Act (1887):
- Goal was to assimilate Native Americans
	- Make them "Americans" and eliminate Native Culture
- Provisions:
	- Native Tribes were Dissolved
	- Heads of families would receive 160 acres of land
- Effects:
	- Many Children were sent to boarding Schools
	- Families were broken up
	- People who didn't agree were sent to small reservations
