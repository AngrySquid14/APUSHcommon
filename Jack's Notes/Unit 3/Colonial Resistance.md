## George Greenville: Imperial Reformer:
- Reducing expenses of empire = Initiate new taxation policies
- Fully integrate colonial America into the Empire
- Impress upon colonists that they had responsibilities to the Empire
- Hold the line on Mercantilism:
	- Reduce smuggling within colonies
	- Slow colonial manufacturing development
- English citizens paid 5x tax -> colonists recognized as citizens
- ##### Acts:
	- Housing act:
		- Colonists had to house soldiers
	- Stamp Act:
		- Tax on colonial documents

## Ideological Roots of Resistance:
- Colonial resistance started in the Seaports
- Patriot thinking was shaped by lawyers drawing on **English Common Law, Enlightenment thinking, and Whig philosophy**
- ##### Tax Resistance took 3 forms:
	- Intellectual Protest
		- -> Publishing newspapers/essays/pamphlets 
	- Economic Boycotts
		- -> Stamp Act Boycott
	- Violent Intimidation
		- -> Tarring & Feathering
- British Lawmaking for the colonies:
	- Based on "Virtual representation"
	- the Parliament was to legislate on their behalf
	- they didn't need a representative
	- **Colonists rejected this as illegitimate**

## Parliament Compromises (1766):
- Colonial protests lead to **Repeal of Stamp Act**
- Parliament passes the **Declaratory Act** in 1766
	- To reassert it's right to tax colonies

## Charles Townshend:
- ##### The Townshend Acts (1767):
	- Taxed imports of paper, paint, glass, tea
	- In order to pay for British military costs and administrative fees
	- Also Provides writs of assistance
	- **Restraining Act** suspended NY legislature -> signaled new attitude toward colonial government
- Colonists protested new taxes through nonimportation agreement
- **AND** homespun cloth and other homemade goods -> created by Daughters of Liberty
	- **Homespun Movement** was a major way that women were able to impact the resistance movement
