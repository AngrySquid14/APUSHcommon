### Teddy Roosevelt (1858 - 1919)
- **26th President**
	- Inaugurated after William McKinley's assassination
	- Youngest President at the time (42)
- **War Hero**
- **Conservationist**
- **Former Governor of NY**
- **Strong, Aggressive Leadership**:
	- Believed that Pres. should be a "steward of the people"
	- "Trustbuster" and worked to resolve conflicts between labor and management
