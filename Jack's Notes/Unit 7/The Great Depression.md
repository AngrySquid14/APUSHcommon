## Overview:
- **Stock Market Boom -> 1920**
	- Businesses grew -> Electricity, Car, Entertainment industries
	- People started buying stocks
		- Was popular to do
		- People were investing in new industries
		- **Many Stocks were Overvalued**
			- Prices were rising very quickly -> **People would use loans to buy stocks** knowing that they would be able to pay it back right away
		- Little to no stock market regulation 
		- Nothing to prevent risky investments/fraud
- ### Causes:
	- #### **Stock Market Crash -> 1929**
		- Sept 3rd 1929 -> stock prices begin to fall
		- Oct 23rd -> Dow Jones dropped avg of $21/hour
		- **Black Thursday => Oct 24th 1929**
			- People started selling stocks rapidly
			- Prices dropped quickly -> people lost $3 billion in a day
		- **Black Tuesday => Oct 29th 1929**
			- More than 16 million shares were sold off
			- **The market collapsed**
	- Issues in agriculture started before the Great Depression
	- Farmers were earning less and were commonly in debt, losing farms
	- #### **The Dust Bowl -> mid-1930s**
		- Drought, Removal of grasses, Overgrazing -> ruined farms in the Great Plains Region
		- Windstorms followed -> large dust clouds -> blocked out the sun and hurt crops
		- **Led to Mass migration of farmers to California**
	- Factory Workers wages were low
	- #### **Banking Issues:**
		- Banks invested lots of the money they held into the stock market.
		- **People who had money in banks raced to claim their money, and when banks closed, people and businesses with money in banks lost access to it**
	- #### **Unemployment:**
		- Businesses laid people off to stay afloat
		- by 1932 12mil Americans were unemployed
		- **Families Impact:**
			- Men were ashamed they couldn't support family -> left home
			- ~250,000 children left home and traveled the country
				- "Wild Boys" or "Hoover Tourists"


**THREE MAIN CAUSES**
-> *STOCK MARKET CRASH*
buying on margin, overspeculation
-> *INTERNATIONAL ECONOMIC PROBLEMS*
high tariffs hurt international trade
-> *BANKING PROBLEMS*
fed. reserve fails, people lose faith in u.s. banks


**Hooverville** => Shanty town (Depression was blamed on Herbert Hoover)
 
## Roosevelt's New Deal:
- FDR wins 1932 election over Hoover
- In first speech promises a *"New Deal"*
- **Goals - 3 R's**
	- Relief -> Direct help for poor, unemployed, struggling
	- Reform -> Changes to avoid future crisis, help restore trust in economy
	- Recovery -> Bringing economy back to normal levels
- **Beginning of American Liberalism** -> Govt. providing social services to the people
