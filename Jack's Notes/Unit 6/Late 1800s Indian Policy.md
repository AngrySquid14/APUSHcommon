### Sand Creek Massacre:
- Massacre of Cheyenne natives after they were forced to move into a different reservation
	- New reservation had no game animals
	- Americans trespassed
	- **Cheyennes returned to old hunting grounds, younger men began to attack stagecoaches**
- **Chivington** sought to get rid of Cheyennes
- **September 1864** -> Peace Chiefs (Black Kettle) met with Colorado -> was promised safety from the army
- Chivington and the Third massacred the Sand Creek reservation:
	- Killed men, women, and children

#### The Sioux Alliance:
- In the late 1800s, the increase in soldiers, miners, and U.S. incursion on to their territory was a threat to Lakota existence
	- **4 Lakota Tribes come together for mutual protection:**
		- Red Cloud - Strategist
		- Crazy Horse - Strong Warrior
		- Sitting Bull - Holy Man
		- Gall
