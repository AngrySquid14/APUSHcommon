**1890** -> Louisiana enacted a law, required railway cars to be segregated

**1892** -> **Homer Adolph Plessy** (1/8 African American) & others wanted to challenge this law
- Plessy was selected -> He was white passing
	- Bought a 1st Class ticket and sat in whites only car
	- Announced he was coloured -> was told to move to black car -> **was arrested**

### Legal Question:
- Aimed to criticize the subjective nature of segregation
- Plessy's lawyer argued that **"separate but equal" wasn't true**
- Supreme court decided that:
	- Segregation was constitutional so long as they were equal
- Made Segregation legal in the whole country -> set up the precedent for the next 30 years.
