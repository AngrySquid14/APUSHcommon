### Barbed Wire:
- Secured animals
- Saved costs on timber -> cheaper than a large fence
- Native Americans didn't like -> thought land wasn't to be owned

### Refrigerated Freight Car:
- Used frequently by Gustavus Swift
- Increased efficiency in transporting goods
	- Cut costs
- Allowed meat to be transported further

### Silo:
- Used for storing grain
- Improved longevity of stored grain
	- Allowed for longer term selling -> more convenient for farmers
- Allowed for selling at higher prices (more opportune timing)

## The Populist Party:
- Formed in the late 19th Century
- Made up of Farmers mostly
- Originated in Cincinnati
- **Sought increased govt. involvement in the economy:**
	- Railroads, Banks, govt. reform
	- Critical of Lases Faire Capitalism
#### Supporters:
- **Farmers:**
	- The Grange -> sought to connect farmers, share techniques
		- Wanted state lawmakers to support their programs
	- Farmers' Alliances -> local organizations, established stores, banks
- **Industrial Workers:**
	- Knights of Labor -> skilled and unskilled workers
		- Terrance Powderly (Founder)
- **Economic Reformers:**
#### Creation:
- **Economic Issues:**
	- High shipping prices on RR hurt farmers
	- Fed govt. gives RR subsidies to expand
	- Was expensive to store goods with RRs
	- **Panics of 1873, 1893:**
		- Crop prices dropped
		- Hurt farmers significantly
- **Emerged from previous farming groups**
	- Granges and Farmers' Alliances
#### Goals:
- **Omaha Platform**
	- Direct election of Senators
	- Graduated Income Tax
	- Australian (Secret) Ballot -> Confidentiality when casting votes
	- Govt. Control of railroads, telegraphs, telephones
	- Free coinage of Silver -> Wanted to be able to pay back debts easier
			- 16 oz of silver = 1 oz of gold
			- would increase money supply (Silver would count towards the value money that can be printed)
			- supported by farmers, debtors
			- opposed by industrialists, lenders 
	- Civil Service Reform
	- 8-Hour Workday
#### Impacts:
- Most ideas came later (During Progressive Era)
- **16th Amendment** -> Income Tax
- **17th Amendment** -> Direct Election of Senators
- **Secret Ballots**
#### Decline:
- Economy changed, Crop prices increased
- Yukon Gold Rush -> more money backed by gold standard
- Farming was more mechanized
- Race divided populist party
- Populists weren't able to break existing party loyalties
- Democratic Party took over their goals

### Election of 1896:
- William Jennings Bryan (D and Populist) **VS** William McKinley (R)
- Populist Party supported Bryan due to his support for Free Silver
- **"Cross of Gold" Speech**
