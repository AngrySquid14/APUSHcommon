## Social Darwinism:
- Charles Darwin -> Theories of natural selection and evolution (Rejected Social Darwinism)
- **Social Darwinism is** the application of natural selection to the development of society
	- Societies progress on a basis of survival of the fittest
- **Used to justify increasing inequality** in industrial American society
- ### Eugenics:
	- "Science" of human breeding
	- argued that people who were less successful ("Unfit") shouldn't be allowed to reproduce
	- Millionaires thought they were best suited to decide who had certain jobs, and marry/reproduce -> they were most "fit"
