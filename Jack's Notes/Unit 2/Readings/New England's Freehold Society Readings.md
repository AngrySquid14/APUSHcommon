## A New Hampshire Will
- #### Primogeniture:
	- The Legal principle that the eldest son inherits all family property
- #### Questions:
	- Nicholas Dudley made sure to give a solid portion of his land to his wife, along with his cattle and other animals, as well as the ability to use and improve all of them. 
		- He likely did this in order to give her a source of income and allow her to not be forced to remarry to live
	- He treats his firstborn son better than his others, and most of the others basically the same
		- He gives his firstborn more land because of primogeniture likely, and gives the rest the same, except for Joseph, who hadn't already received his portion of the estate
	- He does treat his daughters as beneficiaries, as he states that he'll give the land controlled by his wife to his daughters and one grandson in the event of her death or remarriage

## The Obligations of a Wife
- #### Questions:
	- A husband is expected to be gentle, loving, kind and tender towards his wife.
		- He's not supposed to be harsh and treat her like a servant.
		- Any "governance" that's happening should be out of love instead of fear
	- A wife is expected to obey her husband, and submit to them
	- Higher birth/intelligence/estate doesn't alter a wife's obligation
		- She's still supposed to be obedient.
