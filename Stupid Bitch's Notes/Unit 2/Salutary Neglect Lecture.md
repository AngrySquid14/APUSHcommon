## Navigation Acts:
- Major Goals:
	- Increased English Profits
	- Increased Law enforcement
- Limited trade with other countries/colonies:
	- Dutch, French
- Lumber and shipbuilding business was up in colonies:
	- England needed more ships for trade
- Increased taxes:
	- Many colonists smuggled to avoid taxes

## Dominion of New England:
- Mass. Bay colony refused to enforce the Navigation Acts:
	- refused Anglican church -> not puritans
- #### King Charles II made it a royal colony:
	- Revoked Mass. Bay assembly
	- Colonists still refused to pay taxes
- #### King James creates new Royal Province -> Dominion of New England:
	- Appointed Governor Andros

## Glorious Revolution:
- King James II was unpopular in England:
	- Catholic
	- Revoked English town's charters
	- Ignored Parliament
- Parliament forced King James into exile in France:
	- His protestant daughter and her husband (Mary and William) took the crown
	- Lead to the English Bill of Rights

## Colonial Rebellions:
- #### Massachusetts:
	- Militia toppled Andros
	- Broke up rule of Dominion
		- Became separate Royal Colonies
- #### Maryland: 
	- Protestants forced out the Catholic Governor
- #### New York:
	- Jacob Leisler and Dutch Militia take over
		- Push out Lt. Gov. Nicholson
		- Leisler loses favor when he imprisoned opponents and raised taxes
			- Convicted by British Jury and court, and beheaded

## Salutary Neglect:
- Salutary Neglect -> hands off policy of ruling a colony
	- Parent countries still make policies, but don't rule strictly
- #### Causes:
	- Growing British Prosperity:
		- Made them more lax
	- Britain more focused on European affairs
	- Declining enforcement of mercantilist policies:
		- Prime Minister Robert Walpole filled govt. with people who didn't do anything
- #### Effects: 
	- Rise of self-government in 13 colonies
		- Strengthened assemblies
	- colonial economy flourished with less regulations:
		- more trading with other countries
	- Increased self-rule grew colonial autonomy:
		- lead to more independent, American, identities.

## English Models of Govt. in the Colonies:
- Local Govts. more influential than Britain
- Colonial assemblies were bicameral, like English Parliament
	- Governors council -> upper house
		- Each colony had a royal Governor
	- Local Assembly -> lower house

## End of Salutary Neglect (Unit 3)
- Charles Townshend speaks of replacing salutary neglect with stronger control
- French and Indian War officially ends Salutary neglect:
	- Britain passes acts taxing colonies to help with debt
