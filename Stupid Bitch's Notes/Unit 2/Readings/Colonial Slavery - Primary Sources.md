## Doc 3.4 - South Carolina:
- #### Questions:
	- The cultural assumptions of the act are that the slave population is below everyone, and they are naturally barbaric and savage, so they need different rules.
	- The responsibilities of slave owners are, generally to limit all degrees of freedom slaves are given, and to make revolt harder.
		- More specifically they are:
			- To stop slaves when they're seen outside of a plantation and check if they have a ticket allowing them to be out
			- To keep firearms hard to find to prevent easy uprisings.
			- To search the slave housing for stolen/illegal weapons and other things
	- The willingness of slaveholders to carry out these actions implies that they are seriously afraid of a potential slave uprising, because they're potentially throwing away their investment and limiting output by putting slaves through more rigorous processes and enforcing a death penalty.
